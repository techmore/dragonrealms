Current status

The full barbarian identity chain now works end-to-end: hall learning, signature roar landing in its one valid slot, augmentation ranks accruing for the first time, honest rank tracking from exp output, and zero wedges in recent runs. Circle 2 itself has not yet been reached — that remains the goal metric — but the shortfall curve is finally bending instead of flatlining.

Long-term goals

Near term — first circle-up active focus

  • Reach circle 2. The one true goal metric. Every run that ends short is graded by shortfall-closure rate so variants stay comparable without it.
  • Get the readiness gate to fire from expRanks instead of mindstate (mindstate hides fully-converted skills — it has never once triggered a circle attempt).
  • Validate the roarSmart variant A/B (fewer refused roars, cleaner logs, zero exp cost).

Mid term — efficient circling machine

  • Kill rate to 50/h: shorten bazaar errand legs, cut per-trip learn/scan overhead, tighten RT idle in the fight loop.
  • Variance collapse: median run within ~20% of best — kill regressions and wedges for good via sweeps.db trend tracking.
  • Circle 3–5 without redesign: script regeneration already retargets curricula; verify slot counts, ability unlocks, and wider creature bands keep up unattended.
  • Variant graduation rule: a variant replaces baseline when it wins on shortfall-closure rate across ≥6 runs; losing variants are dropped per the "no strength" rule.

Long term — generalizes to every guild

  • Circle 10 barbarian — full weapon ladder (club → short sword → cavalry sabre), armor progression, all four ability classes.
  • Per-guild kaizen kits: each guild gets its own signature-slot rules, train sets, and fidelity checks like barbarian's. plumbing (signatureAfter, abilitySlots, trainSets) is guild-agnostic already.
  • Fidelity ledger as acceptance criteria for all ten remaining guilds: no guild ships without its identity verbs provably firing in live runs.
  • Continuous benchmarking: scheduled baseline runs flag pacing regressions before they compound into measurement drift.

Open risks & honest caveats

  • No circle-up has ever happened — the grade ladder and all pacing projections remain unvalidated against the actual event.
  • The readiness gate is still blind: it reads mindstate which hides fully-converted skills. Until it reads expRanks, every hall trip is timer-driven, not intelligence-driven.
  • Run variance is wide (kills/hour spread ~2x) with few repeats; medians are thin. Kaizen A/Bs use one-variable changes for exactly this reason.
  • Measurement has repeatedly lied to us — truncated fidelity logs, mindstate omissions, latched wedge detectors. The DB + harness probes are ground truth; grep counts alone are not. Eight such traps are catalogued in sim architecture §7.
