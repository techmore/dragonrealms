What changed this arc (muse-c20 p1→p10, r1→r3): circle-2 shortfall went 84 → 10 ranks (88.1% of the gate closed — best ever), on ONE persistent character (vltm) across resumed 45-min legs. The five structural bugs that ate six prior arcs are fixed server- and script-side: refused-roar roundtime pinning, skin-before-corpse, watchdog eating the post-kill window, survival lanes never scheduled, and the purse leaking into rank-163 "hall training". Skinning went 0 → 18 (five legs at literal zero), silver flows, and the last 10 ranks are all purchasable/study-able. The wall is now silver income and exp imbalance — both have named fixes in flight (climbFinish leg running; crier-quest loop measured at ~30 silver/min vs hunting's ~1.7/min).

Spawn availability — solved this arc

The zrmn-era starvation is gone for sweep windows: DR_SPAWN_MULT (the test-only agent boost, ×20 in this arc) scales density and the anti-camp threshold together, and the occupancy ladder spreads agents. Measured pace: 49 kills/h (p1, dibo), 40–44/h typical healthy. The throttle still exists and still matters for crowded live worlds — but it is no longer the sim's binding constraint. Kill-rate work is parked; survival/lore/silver are the current front (Sean's prioritization call, validated by the numbers: all four survival lanes closed mid-leg once scheduled, ~2–3 minutes of roundtime each).

Lock-driven training — weapon, armour, and the climb working

Real players watch mind state and move off a locked skill. The scripts now do the same three things, each measured as its own kaizen change. The sensing is free: put exp already fires at the per-kill checkpoint and prints every skill with its lock word (mind lock), so the generated script branches on reply prose — no new plumbing, no side channels.

Long-term goals

Near term — first circle-up active focus

  • Close the last 10 ranks (vltm): the climbFinish leg is running — teachable-filtered hall training, Academy study detour for both lore rows, gear-ledger kit buys (club/padded cloth/iron helm). Then resume legs accumulate silver until the buys fire.
  • Silver engine — the crier quests: 70–75s per kill-quest (rats already the target), ~30 silver/min vs hunting's ~1.7/min, plus appraisal exp per claim. One generator block, never once used in 600+ runs. Measured candidate for the 6-minute finish (Sean's target).
  • Gear-ledger script standard (built 2026-09-04): every purchasable piece is one declarative row (gearLedger in data/guild-scripts.js) priced from ITEMS[id].value at generation time — no hardcoded prices (the old blocks had already drifted: sleeves gated at 60 vs value 45), no copy-paste blocks per guild. Circle-3+ becomes data-only.
  • Exp-imbalance audit (Sean's call): map each lane's exp income vs its circle need; propose the smallest teaching-band change (passives should back off ~2× past their need). Evasion 163 vs need 6 is the poster child.

Mid term — efficient circling machine

  • Variance collapse: median run within ~20% of best; kill regressions + nav wedges caught via sweeps.db trend tracking.
  • Circle 3–5 without redesign: script regeneration already retargets curricula; verify slot counts, ability unlocks, wider creature bands keep up unattended.
  • Variant graduation rule: a variant replaces baseline when it wins on shortfall-closure rate across ≥6 runs; losing variants dropped per the "no strength" rule.

Long term — generalizes to every guild

  • Circle 10 barbarian — full weapon ladder (club → short sword → cavalry sabre), armor progression, all four ability classes.
  • Per-guild kaizen kits: each guild gets its own signature-slot rules, train sets, fidelity checks. The circling kaizen is already guild-agnostic (it lives in buildCircleScript), so warmage inherits it once it can hunt — see the Warmage roadmap.
  • Fidelity ledger as acceptance criteria for all ten remaining guilds.
  • Continuous benchmarking: scheduled baseline runs flag pacing regressions before they compound.

Open risks & honest caveats

  • No circle-up has ever happened — the grade ladder and all pacing projections remain unvalidated against the actual event. Best recorded circle-2 is still the pre-kaizen 32.9 min. Shortfall 10 is the closest approach ever (88.1% of the gate).
  • Exp imbalance is the new structural risk (Sean, 2026-09-04): passives (evasion 163, fitness 159) gain at full rate forever while the gate rows need deliberate action. If the teaching-band audit doesn't land, every future circle pays a widening junk-rank tax — total ranks are no longer a proxy for progress (1371 ranks vs 88% gated).
  • Silver math is thin: ~370s needed, resume legs bank ~30–90s each after kit spends. The crier-quest loop (~30/min, never used in 600+ runs) is unproven live; skin sales (~4/pelt) plus kill coins currently lose to kit costs on any leg with a death.
  • Run variance is wide (kills/hour spread ~2×) with few repeats; medians are thin. Kaizen A/Bs use one-variable changes for exactly this reason.
  • Measurement has repeatedly lied to us — truncated fidelity logs, mindstate omissions, latched wedge detectors. The DB + harness probes are ground truth; grep counts alone are not. Catalogued in sim architecture §7.
