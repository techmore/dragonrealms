The approach that compounds — how a leg works now

Every --resume leg with the climbFinish variant runs the same six-part loop; each part is independently measured and versioned, and the whole climb is data-driven from gearLedger rows + the circle requirement tables:

Spawn availability — solved this arc

The zrmn-era starvation is gone for sweep windows: DR_SPAWN_MULT (the test-only agent boost, ×20 in this arc) scales density and the anti-camp threshold together, and the occupancy ladder spreads agents. Measured pace: 49 kills/h (p1, dibo), 40–44/h typical healthy. The throttle still exists and still matters for crowded live worlds — but it is no longer the sim's binding constraint. Kill-rate work is parked; survival/lore/silver are the current front (Sean's prioritization call, validated by the numbers: all four survival lanes closed mid-leg once scheduled, ~2–3 minutes of roundtime each).

Lock-driven training — weapon, armour, and the climb working

Real players watch mind state and move off a locked skill. The scripts now do the same three things, each measured as its own kaizen change. The sensing is free: put exp already fires at the per-kill checkpoint and prints every skill with its lock word (mind lock), so the generated script branches on reply prose — no new plumbing, no side channels.

Long-term goals

Near term — circle 7 → 10 active focus

  • Verify the tactics fix (r22): analyze-banked tactics exp should close c7's rank-7 need within one leg; if conversion lags, extend the triple-read pattern to tactics (a second academy verb or a study-grade repeat).
  • TDP → stat spend policy (Sean's request): ~10,500 TDPs banked unspent. Audit which stat raises actually convert to gate speed (agi → trip/brawling chance; wis → study/training rate) and encode a spend curve into the hall trip.
  • Crier-quest silver loop: ~30 silver/min plus appraisal exp per claim; becomes the silver engine when c8+ gear prices outpace gem income. One generator block; never once used in 600+ runs.
  • Exp-imbalance audit (Sean's call): teaching bands should scale with the requirement's rank — evasion at 1,067 ranks is still gaining at full rate. Until then the junk-rank tax compounds every circle.
  • Gear-ledger standard (built, holding): every purchasable piece is one declarative gearLedger row priced from ITEMS[id].value at generation time. Circle-8+ needs only new rows, not new code.

Mid term — the c10 push and variance

  • Circles 7–10 without redesign: the resume machine closes each gate autonomously now; verify ability unlocks (c8+), wider creature bands, and the arena ladder keep pace unattended.
  • Variance collapse: median leg within ~20% of best; kill regressions + nav wedges caught via sweeps.db trend tracking.
  • Variant graduation rule: a variant replaces baseline when it wins on shortfall-closure rate across ≥6 runs; losing variants dropped per the "no strength" rule. climbFinish is the current baseline by this rule.

Long term — generalizes to every guild

  • Circle 10 barbarian — full weapon ladder (club → short sword → cavalry sabre), armor progression, all four ability classes.
  • Per-guild kaizen kits: each guild gets its own signature-slot rules, train sets, fidelity checks. The circling kaizen is already guild-agnostic (it lives in buildCircleScript), so warmage inherits it once it can hunt — see the Warmage roadmap.
  • Fidelity ledger as acceptance criteria for all ten remaining guilds.
  • Continuous benchmarking: scheduled baseline runs flag pacing regressions before they compound.

Open risks & honest caveats

  • Tactics is the c7+ wall (fresh fix): every circle now needs tactics rank = circle number, and its only exp source was successful trips — ~3% vs c6+ spawns. The analyze fix banks exp per read, but if rank conversion lags the pool-drain curve the way lore did, expect one slow leg at c8 before the triple-read pattern applies to it too (watch this space).
  • Exp imbalance is compounding (Sean, 2026-09-04): passives (evasion 1,067, fitness 915) gain at full rate forever while the gate rows need deliberate action. Total ranks (~6,900) are ~13× the gated need — the junk-rank tax widens every circle and total ranks are no longer a proxy for progress. The teaching-band audit (passives back off past their need) is the game-side fix candidate.
  • TDP pool sits unspent (~10,500): the machine banks TDPs but has no stat-spend policy. Whether agi (trip chance), wis (learning speed), or discipline converts best into gate speed is unmeasured — queued audit.
  • Deaths at c5+ (0–2/leg): wraith-class hits + bleed-outs. The post-flee tend/rest interleave holds so far; a third death in one leg should trigger a closer look at the c7+ arena ladder.
  • Silver math now holds but is gem-dependent: gems fund the kit through c7; if gem drops thin at c8+ spawn pools, the crier-quest loop (~30/min, also banks lore) becomes the next silver engine.
  • Measurement has repeatedly lied to us — truncated fidelity logs, mindstate omissions, latched wedge detectors. The DB + harness probes are ground truth; grep counts alone are not. Catalogued in sim architecture §7.
