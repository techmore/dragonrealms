State of the guild: Warrior Mage has never reached circle 2 in any recorded run (0 circle-ups across 31 warmage lab runs). In the zrmn kaizen run (6 agents · 15 min · boost ×20), 2 of 3 warmages wedged at the bazaar with 0 ranks and 0 kills; the third was slow (0 kills, 15 ranks). Warmage cannot yet hunt reliably — the bazaar stall is the #1 wall, ahead of the circling logic.

Current status

Warmage's identity chain is implemented (familiars, four elemental attunements, Summon Weapon, Impede, the 89-slot spell tree pending), but the progression sim can't exercise it: agents drift to the bazaar and park. The circling kaizen (buildCircleScript's drain + re-attempt) is guild-agnostic — it lives in the shared script generator — so warmage inherits it the moment it can hunt. What warmage needs first is the same two upstream fixes barbarian is waiting on: navigation that holds and kill/cast rate.

Key difference from barbarian: barbarian's gate is almost entirely combat-exp ranks (weapon/armor/parry/evasion/survival). The circling kaizen handles TDP conversion identically, but the exp-rank gate closes slower unless a casting loop sustains.

Spawn availability — shared wall

  • Respawn tick every 25s; anti-camp throttle pushes respawns to +120s past ≥14 respawns in 5 min per room ("the hunting here has grown thin").
  • Quiet world has plenty; starvation only under crowd contention. For warmage the immediate limit is the cast cadence, not creatures — but once it hunts, the same density lever (DR_SPAWN_MULT) applies.

Long-term goals

Near term — first reliable hunt active focus

  • Kill the bazaar stall for warmage. Make the origin-gated arrival + bazaar guard hold — warmage is the guild that wedges hardest. Until an agent fights, nothing else can be measured.
  • Stand up a casting loop. A spell rotation that regens mana between casts and lands damage — this is what accrues the magic ranks that are most of the circle-2 gate.
  • Reuse the circling kaizen. It's shared; once warmage reaches a near-closed gate at the hall, drain + re-attempt should fire without new work. Confirm with a real run.

Mid term — efficient circling machine

  • Establish a warmage baseline variant (none exists) — the first thing the leaderboard can rank.
  • Cast-rate tuning: shorten mana idle, widen the spell rotation, verify targeted_magic / summoning accrue without wedging.
  • Variance collapse across ≥6 repeats, same discipline as barbarian.

Long term — generalizes to every guild

  • Circle 10 warrior mage — full 89-slot spell tree, Aethereal Pathways, talisman familiars.
  • Per-guild kaizen kits: warmage gets its own signature-slot rules + fidelity checks (familiars, attunements, Summon Weapon). Plumbing is guild-agnostic already.
  • Fidelity ledger as acceptance criteria for all ten remaining guilds; continuous benchmarking to flag pacing regressions.

Open risks & honest caveats

  • Warmage has no circle data at all — no baseline, no champion, no variant. Every projection for it is pre-measurement.
  • Navigation still regresses hardest here: warmage is the guild that wedges at the bazaar. This must be solved before any other metric means anything.
  • Two pacing dimensions: barbarian's gate is combat-exp; warmage's is mostly mana-gated magic ranks. Even with perfect nav, circle-2 time depends on cast cadence, which is untested.
  • Measurement has repeatedly lied to us — grep counts and truncated logs are not ground truth; the DB + harness probes are. See sim architecture §7.
