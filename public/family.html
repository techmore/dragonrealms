Part I — The generated circle script, section by section

Generated by scripts/lib/script-gen.mjs → buildCircleScript(), run as <base>circle.dr on every guild-hall trip. Sections stamp in this order:

1. HALLTRIP — gem shed
put drop garnet before any movement. Why first: hoarded gems (~19 burden) refuse every move, and the drop used to live in TRAIN — after the walk it was supposed to enable. Catch-22: can't walk to town to sell, can't sell to lose the weight. Drop has no burden gate and gems respawn from loot.
2. Guild-hall trains + TRY_CIRCLE
put train <skill> lines from the live circle-requirement gap, capped (hallTrainCap), teachable-filtered — lore and over-trained lanes don't bleed silver. Then put circle with a Rise,|not yet ready match pair. Why before town: the hall is the first building the agent passes; circle-up is checked the moment it's affordable, not after errands.
3. Survival tail (SURV_*)
forage / hunt bursts with inline combat-block gates and pause 5 after the skin tail. Why pause 5: sling/heavy weapon RT is 4s — pause 3 lost the race and every forage refused (fresh15-20 chain). The WANDER forage site keeps it out of combat RT races.
4. CRIER STOP (market_way) new this week
Origin guard (ifne room bazaar goto CRIER_DONE) → 2-move walk → room gate → optional quest abandon (starved assignment) → put quest → echo CRIER_VISITED → walk back. Emitted BEFORE the kit buys. Why first among errands: the claim pays ~110s ON the trip — the buy lines must read the post-claim purse (aadd: trips arrived with 76-110s, never the helm's 120). Why the guards: a mis-land walk from any other room lands nowhere near the crier and silently no-ops the stop for the whole leg (uvjq), and an unpaired return walk from the wrong room strands every later stop (xecc).
5. KIT EMITTER (emitKitBuys)
Stamps buy/wear blocks from the guild's gearLedger (padded cloth 40s → iron helm 120s → oaken club 112s → wooden shield 70s → sleeves/boots/pants stack), live ITEMS values, worn-gated (matchre Worn:...<noun-tail>|carrying:... + inventory probe), purse-gated (iflt silver <cost>), double wear sends. Why worn-gate on the noun TAIL: shop noun and worn label disagree on articles ("a sturdy oaken club" vs worn text) — tail substrings survive both wordings. Why the inventory probe: fresh22 bought a second pair of sleeves while the first sat unworn.
6. GEM STOP (market_end quartermaster)
Origin guard → walk → per-gid sell with refusal matchers → return walk inside the sells-ran path. Gems only sell at the quartermaster; the return leg lives behind the guard so a skipped walk never fires its paired walk (the xecc lesson).
7. WALKFAIL landing + ERRAND_DONE + return new
WALKFAIL: sits just before ERRAND_DONE:. The engine abandons any failed move chain at this label. Why: moves are relative — one refusal at the tailor desynced every later baked move (anpo), and the engine then ran sells/quests from the wrong room, silently, for the rest of the leg. With the label, the chain lands here and the return path still walks home.
8. Fane detour (TDP spend)
After STUDY_DONE: walk hall→fane (20 moves), train <stat> twice per point, afford-gated by the engine's %tdp token, threshold 150 TDP / cap 5 points, returns via study detour or bazaar errands. Why con first: +2 maxHp per point compounds across the whole c2→c20 climb (+35% HP bar in fresh15) while leg ranks don't. Kit-before-fane (faneHold): the purse funds ledger gear before stats.
9. SHOP ladder + ARMOR/HELM/SHIELD retries (legacy variants)
Pre-ledger hand-copied blocks (helmRetry, armorStack, shieldKit). climbFinish bypasses these; they stay for older variants until each is migrated.
The hunt script (hunt.dr, runs between trips) shares the same skeleton: arm check → travel to arena → SCAN loop — matchre per-species FIGHT blocks (first-match-wins; the quest target is now hoisted to the front), shared-fight body via putrun <base>fight (bounded 4-round re-attack loop, kill-cry-first matchers), skin on TG (target-gone), desperate flee below 15% HP, WANDER when empty. The fight body carries the 3-blind-swings density, roar/analyze/trip, and the SCRIPT-BUSY guard.
