==Notes==
*Fireball has a chance to spawn fire shards after the initial hit; other times it will just slightly burn them as it goes past.  When you cast the spell at a target, the area mechanics start sorting out which other targets to hit. Any targets that are hit before the intended target get a 'whoosh' single strike. The intended target gets a whoosh or a exploding hit. And any targets that remain after the main target is hit get hit with shrapnel.<ref>[[Re:_fireball_targeting_issue_-_03/06/2015_14:25]]</ref> <br/>
