==Notes==
* The '''Whirlwind [[page type is::ability]]''' is a [[guild association is::Barbarian]] only combat [[page type is::ability]] which allows strikes on multiple targets within the weapon's range. With sufficient skill, Whirlwind has the ability to utilize [[Dual Wield|dual wield]]ing, making one attack with each weapon on everything within range.
* It should be noted that there is a large defensive penalty associated with Whirlwind, making this maneuver dangerous to use at level. Many Barbarians feel that this move is intended to be a finishing move primarily, however when combined with our immobilizing Roar [[Banshee's Wail]], or the stunning Roar [[Lash of Torment]], it can be a deadly mid-sequence attack.
* Recently, the defensive penalty was reduced, and many elder Barbarians claim that it is now a viable hunting tool.
* There is also another use for Whirlwind, to remove oneself or others from webbing, such as a [[Ranger]]'s [[Harawep's Bonds]].
* When you knockdown four with [[Earthquake]] (either at a time or cumulatively over several pulses) you have 10s for an improved Whirlwind. The whirlwind is balance neutral, does increased damage, and only does 2s of RT.
* Lastly, and perhaps of most use, Whirlwind will attempt an attack on anything within range, including unseen opponents. If timed right, it can be an effective deterrent against foes with superior {{skill|Hiding}} or invisibility.
