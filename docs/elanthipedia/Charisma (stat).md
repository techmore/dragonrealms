# Charisma (stat)

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}
'''Charisma''' is a combination of a character's persuasiveness, leadership ability, and other factors when dealing with other characters, NPCs, creatures and spirits. Charisma is often an overlooked statistic, but it is said to affect a great number of systems in subtle ways that are not easy to appreciate.<ref>[[Post:Trader Magic - 03/06/2014 - 23:25]]</ref>
==Uses==

===Combat:===
*Increases [[Spirit Health|spirit health]] and therefore the time before [[depart|auto-departing]] after death
*Used offensively in Spirit, Charm, and Fear [[contested spells]]<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Confidence sucks. - 8/17/2010 - 11:38:21]]</ref>
''*Charisma no longer affects the [[Multi_Opponent_skill|multi opponent]] penalty as of [[Combat 3.0]].''

===[[Justice]] System:===
*Increases the odds of success if you {{com|plead|plead}} {{tt|innocent}}. Charisma does not currently reduce your fines.<ref>[[Post:Less and less time to burgle - 09/16/2020 - 19:57]]</ref>
*Increases the chance of evading the guards when a warrant has been issued.<ref>[[Post:Next... - 12/20/2001 - 13:21:39]]</ref>
*Reduces the round time for [[Plead command|plead release]] attempts from the stocks <ref>[[Post:Stocks 2.0 - 4/13/2010 - 22:14:32]]</ref>

===[[Teaching skill|Teaching]] System:===
*The more charisma your students have, the more teaching experience you will earn
*The more charisma your teacher has, the faster you will learn the skill you are trying to learn
*The more charisma you have, the more [[Scholarship skill|scholarship]] you will learn as a teacher or student.
