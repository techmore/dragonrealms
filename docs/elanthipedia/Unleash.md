==Notes==
*The chance to destroy the scroll is not affected by mana.
*Heritage House members have a [[Sect Spell Affinity|sect affinity]] for this spell giving them a reduced chance to destroy a [[scroll]].
*It is not possible to Unleash a signature spell from a scroll even if that spell is from your own guild.
*The scroll must be held in order to be Unleashed.
*The amount of mana the Unleashed spell is prepared at depends on how much mana you put into Unleash.
:''E.g.: Unleashing Air Bubble with a min prep Unleash prepared it at 5 mana, but using a 60 mana Unleash prepared it at 70 mana.
*Note that the Unleashed spell will be cast at native mana difficulty, but with reduced effectiveness on non-native spells
:''E.g.: Unleashing Wolf's Scent with 89 mana resulted in a +12% buff to Outdoorsmanship, as expected of a Somewhat Dangerous 50% mana efficiency penalty.''

* Chance to destroy seems to be around 4.6%, regardless of scroll mana type or difficulty. However, standard deviation is quite large -- scrolls can be destroyed on the first unleash, or it may take over a hundred tries.   (Sample size: 141, average: 21.65, stdev: 20.5) [Zero difference between ease burden [intro] and imbue [intermediate] data sets, negligible difference with random spell data set, oddly enough necro spells averaged 24.2]
* Chance to destroy with Heritage House sect seems to be around 1% (Sample size: 75, average: 95.25, stdev: 106)
