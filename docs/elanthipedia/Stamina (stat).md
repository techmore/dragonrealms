# Stamina (stat)

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}

'''Stamina''' is a character's ability to perform strenuous activities (such as fighting) over a long period of time.
==Uses==

* Increases [[Vitality]] pool and speed of vitality recovery

===Combat:===

*Increases [[fatigue]] recovery<ref>[[Post:Stamina regen out of combats seems too low. - 01/22/2013 - 02:53]]</ref> and [[Fatigue]] pool size.<ref>[[Post:TEST fatigue pt 1 - 6/27/2009 - 15:42:27]]</ref>
*Reduces [[Fatigue]] cost when completing combat actions.<ref>[[Post:MB stamina costs? - 01/20/2013 - 22:11]]</ref><ref>[[Post:TEST fatigue pt 1 - 6/27/2009 - 15:42:27]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Combat Updates - 4/23/2007 - 1:46:22]]</ref>
*Increases [[Vitality]] and, indirectly, reduces damage from completed attacks.<ref>[[Post:Kodius - Combat 3.0 Guru - 08/08/2012 - 19:05]]</ref>
*Improves [[Evasion]] skill indirectly by reducing [[Encumbrance]]
*Increases accuracy of certain [[Brawling skill|brawling]] commands.
*Used offensively in Power [[contested spells]]<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>
*Used defensively in Fortitude [[contested spells#Defending from Spells|defenses]].<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>


===Guild Specific:===

*Used by [[Bard]]s for [[Bardic Screams]]<ref>[[Post:Bard Teaching 031411 Log - 3/15/2011 - 00:43:00]]</ref>
*Used by [[Empaths]] to assist with [[Empath_Healing|Healing]]<ref>[[Post:FoC Feedback - 1/18/2010 - 20:10:04]]</ref> and [[Manipulate ability|Manipulation]] abilities<ref>[[Post:Manipulation Updates - 3/1/2011 - 2:32:59]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Tentative Empath Magic 3.0 Spell List - 2/8/2011 - 23:27:53]]</ref> indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used by [[Moon Mages]] to improve the ability with [[Astral Travel]] indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used by [[Thieves]] to increase the duration of [[khri]] <ref>[[Post:OOC_Thief_Meeting_Log_12.14.2008_-_12/14/2008_-_18:46:07]]</ref><ref>[[Post:First Draft New Ambush/Khri Charts (from thief OOC Meeting) - 06/13/2013 - 14:09]]</ref><ref>[[Post:3.0 stuff - 10/25/2013 - 13:08]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Khri Effects in Magic 3.0 - 8/9/2010 - 11:15:40]]</ref>indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used by [[Traders]] to improve their [[Speculate command|Speculates]] indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used by [[Warrior Mage]]s to increase their maximum [[Summoning_skill|elemental charge]] <ref>[[Post:Pathways - 12/03/2012 - 12:32]]</ref> and to improve their ability to control [[Familiars]] indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].

===Other Uses:===
*Increases maximum [[Encumbrance]]
*Increases [[concentration]]<ref>[[Post:OOC_Thief_Meeting_Log_12.14.2008_-_12/14/2008_-_18:46:07]]</ref>
* Increases resistance to [[damage#disease|Disease]] and [[Damage#Poison|Poison]]
*improves the ability to use [[gwethdesuans]] indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used in Spell casting indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].<ref>[[Post:Tentative Empath Magic 3.0 Spell List - 2/8/2011 - 23:27:53]]</ref><ref>[[Post:The Physiology of Spellcasting - 11/20/2009 - 14:54:15]]</ref>
*Assists with [[Athletics]] for climbing, swimming and retreating<ref>[[Post:Climbing 3.0 - 10/29/2008 - 20:41:01]]</ref> indirectly by increasing [[fatigue]] and [[concentration]].
