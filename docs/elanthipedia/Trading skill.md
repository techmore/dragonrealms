# Trading skill

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

Trading is the special [[page type is::skill]] of the [[guild association is::Trader]] guild.  It allows Traders to get better deals from merchants, better contract deals, and many other Trader-specific benefits.  The amount that you earn upon completion of the following acts is determined in part by the value of the transaction that is taking place and what kind of transaction it is, all with consideration to your current level.  As such, the greater your [[charisma]], the greater amount that you receive financially per transaction, and thus, potentially, the greater the experience rewarded as well.

Non-traditional ways to train Trading:
* selling branches to [[Mags]]
* selling items at the pawn shop
* completing foraging and skinning [[Tasks]]
** Foraging tasks give 1-5 mindstates of Trading per completion, depending on how many items are requested.
** 5 item tasks give 1 mindstate, 6-8 give 2, 9-11 give 3, 12-14 give 4, and 15 give 5.
** Each request size has an equal chance of being offered, resulting in an average of 3 mindstates per completed task.
* asking your [[tessera]] about the INVEST deal

Traditional ways to train Trading:
* moving contracts for the guild
** Complete [[Task#Trading|Trading tasks]] for an additional bonus
* selling commodities
* completing [[work orders]]. The amount of EXP granted is based on the number of items included in and difficulty of the work order (quality of materials have no effect).
* selling bundles, gems, and gem pouches
''Note:'' Selling bundles and gems is potentially lucrative for both parties due to the [[Trader gem selling bonus]]. Selling individual gems does not teach any Trading, only gem pouches themselves.
