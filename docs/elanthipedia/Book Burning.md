==Notes==
* This is a ritual spell that will create a spectral torch. Meant to be a significant part of a Redeemed's damage output, it has a chance to fire TM attacks following any single-target offensive spell you cast. The torch can be worn or held -- the latter will lend added accuracy to its attacks.
:* {{com|study}} {{tt|torch}} (held) - ''You think you can WEAR the torch, forgoing some of the accuracy of its attacks.''
:* {{com|study}} {{tt|torch}} (worn) - ''You think you can REMOVE or HOLD the torch, lending added accuracy to its attacks.''
:* {{com|wear}} {{tt|torch}} - Moves the torch from your hand to floating near you.
:* {{com|remove}}/{{com|hold}} {{tt|torch}} - Moves the torch from floating near you to your hand. This prioritizes the left hand if available.
* Activation chance is higher against cursed targets. Against cursed/undead creatures, activation is guaranteed. Against cursed players, it depends on their level of Divine Outrage or soul corruption.
* The torch's attack is separate from the cast that triggers it, occurring after a slight delay.
* The torch will pull the caster from hiding when it attacks.
* The torch will occasionally say part of a zealous Redeemed mantra when it attacks.
* Provides lighting to the room.
* You may obtain an Anabasis ritual focus by bringing a Maelshyvean hierophant's tentacle to the Redeemed "guild leader."
* Like all other [[:Category:Anabasis spellbook|Anabasis spells]], this is not taught by the various guildleaders.
* Like all other Anabasis spells, this spell only functions while [[Redeemed]].
* The torch is visible in your {{com|look}} but not in the room description.

==Justice==
* The spell will trigger justice mechanics on cast.
* Attacks by the torch will also trigger justice mechanics.
* Walking around or interacting with NPCs while the torch is active does not trigger justice mechanics.
