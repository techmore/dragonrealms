==Notes==
*Self-cast, Seer's Sense provides a direct bonus to evasion skill. 
*Members of the [[Fortune's Path]] have a [[Sect Spell Affinity|spell affinity]] for this spell giving them a duration bonus to the self-cast effect.
:*Non-Gypsies: 10-40 minutes
:*Gypsies: 30-90

*Cast on another, it establishes a link to them, which along with telling you various things about them when you PERCEIVE works with several other spells, including:
:* [[Thoughtcast]] - Anyone you have a Seer's link with will hear any Thoughtcasts you receive
:* [[Thoughtcast]] - An active Seer's link makes Thoughtcasting to the target much easier.
:* [[Riftal Summons]] - A Seer's link makes it easier to cast Riftal Summons on someone by about a one [[Zone Map|zone]] increase in range.
:* [[Contingency]] - You can use Contingency on someone you have a Seer's link with in place of a moonbeam.
*{{com|release}} {{tt|link}} will cancel the link between the caster and target.
*The duration of the spell when cast on others is greater: 30-90
