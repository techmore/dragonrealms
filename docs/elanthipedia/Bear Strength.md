=== Notes ===
* Unlike [[Cheetah Swiftness]], this spell does not have any cyclic messaging.
* This cyclic spell pulses protection from crowd control: stunned, immobile, webbed, calmed, slept
** Immobilize protection: XXX's torso muscles ripple with power as he/she unleashes a mighty roar! 
** Stun protection: XXX shake his/her head viciously from side to side, breaking free of the stun! 
** Web protection: XXX rip free of his/her bonds wih a mighty roar! 
** Does not protect against sleep/unconscious.
*Per GM Grejuva, the spell pulses very fast (twice as fast as Awaken) with a percentage chance to clear the status, percentage based on potency.  Much higher chance to remove webs.
