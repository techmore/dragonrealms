== Notes ==
*Spell cannot be re-cast if the caster has an existing innocence on them. Can be re-cast after releasing the spell {{tt|Release INNOCENCE}} or after it expires.
*Prevents facing and advancing by players and creatures, but does not stop ranged attacks such as arrows, thrown weapons and spells. Also prevents the use of {{tt|AIM}} with ranged attacks.
*Facing or advancing by the caster will cause the spell to wear off.
*May enrage [[undead]].
