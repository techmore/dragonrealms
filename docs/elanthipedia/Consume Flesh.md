==Notes==
* Must perform the [[Consume ritual|{{tt|consume}} ritual]] before using.
* Can be used on dead [[PC|player characters]].
* Syntax is {{com|CAST}} {{tt|[BODY PART]}}.  See also a [[Empath_Healing#List_of_body_parts|complete list of body parts]].
* Casting on a badly damaged part will reduce the spell potency, casting on a destroyed part will cause the spell to fail.
* The number of times Consume Flesh may be cast on a single corpse is based on your {{skill|Thanatology}}, and you gain one additional use every 75 ranks up to a total of 5 uses.
