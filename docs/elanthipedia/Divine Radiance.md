==Notes==
*When cast on yourself it provides a light source.
**This version of the spell acts as a standard Utility spell with a 20 second prep time.
**Can be placed into the [[Osrel_Meraud_3.0|Osrel Meraud]] orb.
*When cast on a creature or player it unbalances/knocks them down and amplifies damage from blessed weapon attacks.
**To use this form of the spell on a target, cast it as though it was a targeted magic spell. E.g. {{com|TARGET}} {{tt|dr}}, {{com|cast}}. This has a 10 second target time and behaves like a battle spell.
**The holy damage amplification works on any type of creature, not just undead/cursed. It effectively adds a holy flare to any holy based damage on the target while the effect lasts. E.g. a blessed weapon strike or a holy tm spell. This damage amplification only lasts for a very short duration.
**This teaches {{skill|Targeted Magic}} if cast on an appropriately skilled target and {{skill|Utility}} if cast near personal spell cap, and will teach both if both conditions are met.
**Does not cause empathic shock
