==Notes==
* Produces a sphere of light which can hold [[vitality]]. The Paladin can sacrifice vitality onto the sphere by {{com|RUB}}ing it. Anyone can then {{com|TOUCH}} the sphere to receive the sacrifice and gain a vitality boost. (Each {{tt|RUB}} puts in 10%.)
* The caster is no longer penalized for drawing vitality out of their own vessel.
* The vessel can be {{com|TURN}}ed to set it follow the Paladin around rather than remain in the room where it was cast.
:* This is a toggle, so the Paladin can turn it on and off as desired.
:* The vessel is slow, please give it a few seconds to catch up to you after you move.
