==Notes==
*Only full preparation is allowed.
*Cannot be released while damaged.
*Replace casts will only repair the barrier if the rod has broken down or if the new rod is stronger. The duration will be refreshed regardless, however. Damaged rods will need to be repaired via REPAIR ROD
*Requires unobscured starlight, an unobscured bright moon (Xibar or Yavash), or [[Starlight Aura]]. [[Trader spell requirement is::starlightaurabrightmoon| ]]
*While [[Enrichment]] is active, Iridius Rod will become resistant to cold damage as well.
