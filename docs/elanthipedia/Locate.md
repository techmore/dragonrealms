==Notes==
*Requires [[Clear Vision]] to be active.
*In addition to the direct effect of locating a player, this also creates a lingering effect in the room you located, the strength of which differs based on the success of your locate:
:*Partial success: The lingering effect can be [[Backtrace|backtraced]] by a skilled Moon Mage and acts as a room-based anchor for the [[Riftal Summons]] spell.
:*Full success: The same, plus the lingering effect enables the located player to be used as an anchor for [[Shift Moonbeam]].
:Success can be prevented by a target's use of [[Anti-Locate]] items.
