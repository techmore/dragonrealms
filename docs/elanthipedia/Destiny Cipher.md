==Other messaging==
Creatures of light burst into being about you, a disparate collection of shapes and forms with only the barest nod to anthropomorphism.  Battle lines are swiftly drawn and an inevitable conflict is set to unfold before you.  Moments before they can come to blows, an invisible force sweeps the field, sending the horde tumbling into a glowing tangle of limbs. The figures slowly deform, melting and merging until a single androgynous figure arises.<br>
<br>
Minute disturbances in the darkness send rings of ripples dancing across the surface.  They collide, merge, and pass on.  Peaks and troughs accent and cancel one another in a series of chaotic collisions.  As you watch, a sense of harmony arises from their interplay, the ripples drifting into phase and the darkness moving with a unified purpose.<br>
<br>
Countless wisps of light sprout in the darkness most are swiftly extinguished.  Soon, only six remain in a circle about you.  Six slender pillars of light, their surface slicked with condensation.  You focus on each of these in turn, bending them towards you, uniting them into a frail hemispherical shelter under which you sit.<br>
<br>
Your mind is cleared of distraction, the present and the mundane.  You are adrift in an unbroken sea of silent darkness.  As time draws out, a muted, yet persistent drone of alien chatter arises inquiries into your nature, declarations of identity, accusations, unfiltered emotions and abstract impressions all assault your tranquility.  These, too, you rebuff as you return to the darkness.<br>
<br>

You hone in on one voice at a time, memorizing its unique cadence before shifting your focus to the next voice.  As you come to know their message, their words, their intent, you realize the throng of voices is really one voice, one language, one truth."<br>
