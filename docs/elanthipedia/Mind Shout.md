==Notes==
*Triggers the [[:Category:Heavy offensive abilities|heavy offensive magic]] cooldown. The base cooldown appears to be 5 minutes. This cooldown can be lowered with {{skill|Debilitation}} and by learning the [[Magical feats|Debiliation Mastery feat]].
* Mind Shout applies all of the effects of Mental Blast. This means that it can deal increased nerve damage, knock its targets out, and cause them to drop held items.
*The spell also messages to your neighbouring rooms although they are not affected. (A unearthly scream echoes from the <adjacent room of caster>.)
*Causes charges of disturbing the peace when cast in justice areas.
