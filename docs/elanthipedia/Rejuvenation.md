==Notes==
*Only works while the target is still dead.
*Once completely restored (silver nimbus), memories will not decay for a time based on the amount of mana used. This decay protection can be refreshed by recasting before the decay sets in again.
*A cleric knowing the [[Infusion ability]] can {{com|INFUSE}} {{tt|MEMORY <AMOUNT OF SPIRIT>}} while dead to protect their own memories at the cost of spirit. This will result in a faster auto-depart, but if you are about to {{com|depart}} it will save your memories.
*Once a target has Vigil on him or her you can rejuve them from another room. Handy if they're getting healed in a bad mana room, you can move to a better room and rejuve them. (see talk page for example)
