==Notes==
*Valid targets are [[Katamba]], [[Xibar]], [[Yavash]], or a player character you have an active [[Seer's Sense]] link with. In all cases the teleport is still self only. 
**If using a Seer's Sense link then a moon is still required, but it doesn't matter which one as long as one is available at the time the teleport is triggered.
*You may {{com|INVOKE}} {{tt|Contingency}} when you have the Contingency spell on you to manually trigger the spell.
*Has the same requirements and effects as [[Teleport]], including distance modifiers, stuns, and moon requirement, except that a [[Seer's Sense]] link can be used as an end-point instead of a moonbeam if desired.
*Teleporting between the islands of Qi'Reshalia requires more mana, and teleporting between the mainland and the islands requires even more. This uses a similar modifier calculation to [[Moongate]].
*{{skill|Utility}} determines the odds of the spell triggering.
*[[Braun's Conjecture]]'s effects are checked at the time of casting the spell and not at the time of the teleport triggering.
