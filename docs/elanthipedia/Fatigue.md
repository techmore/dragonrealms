# Fatigue

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

[[Fatigue]] affects how often your character can perform a strenuous action.


==Formulas==

A character's fatigue pool size is determined by primarily by [[Stamina]] and to a much lesser degree by [[Discipline]]<ref>[[Post:Stamina regen out of combats seems too low. - 01/22/2013 - 02:53]]</ref><br/>
Fatigue recovery (per pulse) is determine primarily by [[Stamina]] and secondarily by [[Discipline]]<br/>
Fatigue cost is determined primarily by [[Stamina]] and secondarily by [[Strength]]<ref>[[Post:TEST fatigue pt 1 - 6/27/2009 - 15:42:27]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Combat Updates - 4/23/2007 - 1:46:22]]</ref><ref>[[Post:MB stamina costs? - 01/20/2013 - 22:11]]</ref>

==Activities that affect fatigue==
===Fatigue Usage===
*[[Athletics]] for climbing, swimming and retreating<ref>[[Post:Climbing 3.0 - 10/29/2008 - 20:41:01]]</ref>
*Attacking with a weapon (Fatigue drain increases with weapon weight)
*[[Brawling]]
*Casting [[spells]]
*[[Empaths]] when [[Empath_Healing|transferring]] wounds and casting [[Fountain of Creation]] <ref>[[Post:FoC Feedback - 1/18/2010 - 20:10:04]]</ref>
*[[RTS|Ranger trails]]

===Fatigue Recovery===
*[[Bob_command|BOB]] Command ('''BOB''' regenerates some fatigue according to DR-KODIUS)<ref>[[Post:Stamina regen out of combats seems too low. - 01/22/2013 - 02:53]]</ref>
*Waiting a few moments after a weapon roundtime ends will regenerate 2/3rds of the fatigue used in that attack<ref>[[Post:Strength_and_Stamina_-_01/26/2013_-_02:28]]</ref>
*Sitting or lying prone will recover fatigue twice as fast as standing (Note: Do not try this in combat!)<ref>[[Post:Stamina regen out of combats seems too low. - 01/22/2013 - 02:53]]</ref>
*Being in combat will recover fatigue at half the speed of being outside of combat.<ref>[[Post:Stamina regen out of combats seems too low. - 01/22/2013 - 02:53]]</ref>
