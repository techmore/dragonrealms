==Misconceptions==

There is a myth that the spell Awaken helps to save patients who are "in shock and dying." This was confirmed false by GM [[Naohhi]] in a forum post on 1/11/2015<ref>[[Post:Awaken and the Dying State - 01/11/2015 - 21:28]]</ref>.
<blockquote>While I sometimes enjoy reading the stupid that sprouts up..<br>
<br>
Awaken<br>
*Attempts to cure the states known as Unconscious and Stunned.<br>
*Does nothing to the state known as Dying state that may also be referred to as Pre Death Shock, not to be confused with Empathic Shock.<br>
<br>
Using Awaken on someone in the dying state is as useless as offering lavender to [[Meraud]]. Meaning, you can do it, but those of us that know better are just laughing at you while you make yourself look silly.</blockquote>
<br>
When asked whether Awaken ''ever'' had any effect on the dying state, GM [[Naohhi]] responded:
<blockquote>there was never a bug that did anything like that.<br>
Awaken has not changed since it was released.<br>
I looked at the code before I posted.</blockquote>
