==Notes==
* This spell summons a spirit creature that will react to a spell cast at the cleric by afflicting its caster with a [[Huldah's Pall]] equivalent afterwards (preventing further spellcasting by the caster). It doesn't act as a barrier and cannot react too often.
* Can be placed into the [[Osrel Meraud]] orb.
* Can be infused, causing the spirit creature to leave it's host, allowing spellcasting from that host or to seek a new target.
* Similar to Shadow Servants, these creatures change type/prefix based on your character name. (Blind, Solemn, etc). This is purely a fluff effect, and has no bearing on it's effectiveness.
* Your Sanyu Lyba spirit will automatically point and detect hidden targets in the room for you, based on your perception.
* The noun of the summoned spirit varies based on alignment.
#Light Immortal = house spirit
#Neutral Immortal = watchsoul
#Dark Immortal = phantom thrall
