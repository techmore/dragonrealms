==Notes==
*This spell has four tiers of effects based on mana:
:Tier 1 (any mana): Lets you cast spells that require starlight without regard to weather. I.e. cloud cover will not cause a Starlight Aura cost for starlight based spells cast at night. This does not apply to spells that require moonlight.
:Tier 2 (25% max potency): Regenerate Starlight Aura without regard to weather.  There is a penalty to regen in inclement weather, which goes down with mana invested.
:Tier 3 (50% max potency or greater): Regenerate Starlight Aura without regard to time of day.
:Tier 4 (max mana): Easter egg.
* Noumena can be cast indoors but you will not regenerate [[Starlight Aura]] until you're outdoors. [[Trader spell requirement is::none| ]]
* Noumena's regenerative effects do not extend indoors. Indoors rooms with windows or a view of the sky still count as indoors.
* If you're unsure if you're indoors, use the {{com|weather}} command:
:''That's a bit hard to do while inside.'': Indoors.
:''You glance outside.'': Indoors, with a portal to see out.
:''You glance up at the sky.'': Outdoors
* Heavy cloud cover in inclement weather requires more mana in your spell cast to regenerate SLA (Starlight Aura).
:Note that during some inclement weather, snow for example, clouds won't be mentioned. Assume maximum cloud cover.
