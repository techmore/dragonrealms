# Bardic Lore skill

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

Bardic Lore is the new name of this skill, which will eventually include more than what Music Theory used to.
Bardic Lore is a primary skill of the bards used with their unique music based abilities.It is presently not required for circle purposes, although it is used for the same Titles that Music Theory used to be required for.
Bardic Lore is now the exclusive domain of the [[Bard]] guild, although in the past the Music Theory skill was open to all.

==Training strategies==
Bards learn the {{tt|scream havoc}}, {{tt|scream concussive}} and {{tt|practice breathing}} abilities upon joining the guild. As you progress, various other abilities will be learned.

===[[Bardic Screams]]===
Using any scream in combat will train [[Performance skill|Performance]] and Bardic Lore.  One SCREAM CONCUSSIVE per creature should be enough to mind lock Bardic Lore relatively quickly for most Bards who are hunting at level. 

===[[Bardic Whistling]]===

===[[Segue ability|Segue]]===
Segueing between cyclic spells will yield Bardic Lore experience.

===[[Practice]]===
*Practice Breathing grants a relatively significant amount of Bardic Lore experience.
*Practice Stretching grants a relatively significant amount of Bardic Lore experience.
*Practice Assessment provides a fraction of the experience of Practicing Breathing but can be used much more often.

===[[Song Scrolls]]===
Upon successful memorization of a song from a [[song scroll]], a Bard gets a moderately-sized sum of Bardic Lore experience.

===[[Recall]]/[[Evoke]]===
Use of the {{com|Recall}} command and {{com|Evoke}} command in locations imprinted by the past.

===[[Bluff]]===
* {{tt|Bluff Amaze}} grants a small sum of Bardic Lore experience when used.
* {{tt|bluff distract}} grants a small sum of Bardic Lore experience when used.

===Tuning a Tuneable Instrument===
*{{com|Tune|Tuning}} a tuneable instrument every ~90 seconds will grant Bardic Lore and [[Performance skill|Performance]] experience.

===Classes===
Listening to other Bards teach Bardic Lore classes.
