==Notes==
*The spell selects random targets in the sky for you to {{com|observe}}. After you observe the object, the spell will select a new target on the next pulse for you to observe. This repeats until the ritual ends.
**For each object the spell selects, the timer for observing celestial objects is not enforced (It is enforced for other observations).
**Observing the constellations that the spell indicates reinforces each successive observation, adding an additional amount of prophetic insight. This includes the potential of adding it to a pool that the object may not otherwise fill on its own.
::''E.g. The Magpie normally only fills the defense pool, but the following observation filled both the defense and survival pools:
:::''The Magpie is unobscured by clouds.
:::''The Magpie, symbol of Alamhif, is found year-round in the center of the Starry Path.
:::''The insight gained from your prior observations floats gently through your subconsciousness.  You intuitively draw the relevant details to the forefront of your mind, using them to nurture your current efforts.
:::''You learned something useful from your observation.
:::''You believe you've learned all that you can about survival.
*Casting this spell requires a Perception spellbook focus.
** The [[Item:Tiny_cream-colored_sack|tiny cream-colored sack]] will work with RtR, but has limited uses.
** The [[Magical_feats#Spell_Casting|Improvised Rituals]] magical feat may be required if you are using an unlimited use focus, such as the [[Weapon:Crystal-inset_oaken_staff_surmounted_with_a_witch_ball|crystal-inset oaken staff surmounted with a witch ball]].
*RtR pulses slower during the day than at night.
*It bypasses the telescope requirement to observe for constellations it selects. If you elect to not use a telescope, you will lose a slight bonus to filling your prediction pools.
*Does not work to bypass cloud cover, so it remains beneficial to use in conjunction with [[Piercing Gaze]] and to a lesser degree with a telescope.
*Uses [[:Category:Perception spellbook|Perception spellbook]] mana levels to determine attunement costs.
*There are several restrictions designed to balance letting you fill your prediction pools much faster than you otherwise might be able to:
**There is a one hour cooldown after the ritual ends before you can cast RtR again.
**You must remain outdoors, in view of the sky and kneeling/sitting.
***If you stand while the spell is in effect; it will immediately end the spell and trigger the hour cooldown.
**If you attempt to cast RtR indoors it will consume your concentration for the cast but will not trigger the one hour cooldown.
**[[Teleologic corruption]] increases the difficulty of casting the spell. The potency and duration of the effect is also further reduced beyond the normal effect at the reduced personal mana cap. 
::''E.g. if you're normally able to put ~700 mana into the spell without corruption then you will only be able to put ~450 mana into it with capped corruption and the effect will only be that of an ~100 mana cast.
*A Wrayth script to make observations with the RTR pulse can be found [[RTR_(script)|here]].
