# Debilitation skill

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}

Debilitation is a supernatural/magic skill introduced in [[Magic 3.0]], which governs your ability to reduce the capabilities of others without causing direct damage to cause negative [[status effect]]s.  Success is determined via a [[Contested abilities|stat contest]].  Debilitation is trained in combat, by casting spells on enemies utilizing skill caps comparable with other combat skills.
