*Knowledge of this metaspell, or any other [[elemental weapon]] metaspell increases your weapon to tier 3 from its base of tier 2.
*{{com|SUMMON}} {{tt|WEAPON}} {{tt|FIRE}} or {{com|SHAPE}} {{tt|<weapon> TO FIRE}}
*This converts 100% of the weapon's highest physical damage stat into fire damage.
*To return the weapon to its stone form use {{com|shape}} {{tt|weapon <stone/earth/earthen>}}
*Summoning or shaping a fiery weapon remains elementally aligned to earth, the same as the base ability.
