==Notes==
* Allows you to cleanse the damage stored by [[Emuin's Candlelight]].
::RELIGHT       - cleanse the damage stored by Emuin's Candlelight
::RELIGHT CHECK - display your charge and cooldown status. 
* The spell has 2 charges and regains 1 charge every 15 minutes. However, cooldown will be paused if EMC is not active and your reactor slots are otherwise in use.
* Since the stored damage can persist beyond Emuin's Candlelight ending, these actions still function whether Emuin's Candlelight is active or not.
* As a secondary effect, knowing this spell will greatly increase how much damage Emuin's Candlelight can store.
* Usable while hidden, webbed, or immobile.
* There is variable messaging when using RELIGHT to cleanse stored damage. The first and third lines about entering and exiting the memory are always the same, but the middle line about the memory itself will be randomly chosen from this (as yet incomplete) list:
:*''A door slowly swings open, rusty hinges protesting loudly, and you stride through, limping slightly and head held high.  You find yourself in a softly lit chapel, candles casting dancing shadows along the walls.  At your entrance, the muted sounds of birds nesting in the rafters fall silent, all eyes on you.
:*''Quailing at what you almost remember, you turn back from the horrors you refuse to relive.
:*''Torch in hand, you squint with your one eye as you proceed down a dark corridor.  There are several, unnaturally large insects of different kinds in retreat, but they cannot outrun you.  Taking a prayerful breath, you torch them all.
* Like all other [[:Category:Anabasis spellbook|Anabasis spells]], this is not taught by the various guildleaders.
* Like all other Anabasis spells, this spell only functions while [[Redeemed]].
