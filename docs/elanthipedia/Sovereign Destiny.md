==Notes==
*Has some devastating side effects, mostly to Moon Mages.
**Firstly it will end all active predictions, or spells that require the Plane of Probability to work (specifically: enlightened geometry or teleological sorcery spells, plus Seer's Sense).
**Secondly it will block the casting of these spells as well as any prediction attempts for the duration.
**Trying to predict while under the effects:<br />
**:''You reach towards the Plane of Probability, but find... nothing.  While you are currently free of destiny, it too is free of you.
