# Discipline (stat)

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}

'''Discipline''' is a character's ability to use their mind to control their body.
==Uses==

===Combat===
*Increases [[fatigue]] recovery<ref>[[Post:Stamina regen out of combats seems too low. - 01/22/2013 - 02:53]]</ref> and, to a lesser degree, [[Fatigue]] pool size.<ref>[[Post:TEST fatigue pt 1 - 6/27/2009 - 15:42:27]]</ref>
*Increases [[Spirit Health|spirit health]] and therefore the time before [[depart|auto-departing]] after death
*Improves  skill at blocking with a [[shield usage skill|shield]]
*Used offensively in Mind, Magic, Charm, and Fear [[contested spells]]<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>
*Used defensively in Fortitude and Will [[contested spells#Defending from Spells|defenses]].<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>
*Increases the accuracy of [[Targeted Magic]] spells.<ref>[[Post:3.0 release - 01/18/2013 - 14:50]]</ref><ref>[[Post:New Warrior Mage - 7/28/2010 - 16:53:55]]</ref>


===Guild Specific:===

*Used by [[Bard]]s for [[Bardic Screams]]<ref>[[Post:Bard Teaching 031411 Log - 3/15/2011 - 00:43:00]]</ref> and [[Recall|recalls]]<ref>[[Post:Recall 3.0 - 01/19/2013 - 11:01]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Bard Ability - Evoke - 09/29/2011 - 13:42]]</ref>
*Used by [[Barbarians]] for [[:Category:Barbarian_Forms|forms]], [[meditations]]<ref>[[Post:Circle Reqs - 03/16/2012 - 13:15]]</ref> and [[Roars]]<ref>[[Post:More questions from little barb - 06/25/2014 - 21:44]]</ref>
*Used by [[Empaths]] to assist with [[Empath_Healing|Healing]]<ref>[[Post:FoC Feedback - 1/18/2010 - 20:10:04]</ref> and [[Manipulate ability|Manipulation]] abilities<ref>[[Post:Manipulation Updates - 3/1/2011 - 2:32:59]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Tentative Empath Magic 3.0 Spell List - 2/8/2011 - 23:27:53]]</ref> indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used by [[Moon Mages]] to improve the ability with [[Astral Travel]] indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used by [[Necromancers]] to Increase the number of times [[Spiteful Rebirth]] can be used in a 24 hour period<ref>[[Post:Death and You - 11/21/2009 - 21:44:36]]</ref> indirectly by increasing [[Spirit Health]]
*Used by [[Thieves]] to increase the duration of [[khri]] <ref>[[Post:OOC_Thief_Meeting_Log_12.14.2008_-_12/14/2008_-_18:46:07]]</ref><ref>[[Post:First Draft New Ambush/Khri Charts (from thief OOC Meeting) - 06/13/2013 - 14:09]]</ref><ref>[[Post:3.0 stuff - 10/25/2013 - 13:08]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Khri Effects in Magic 3.0 - 8/9/2010 - 11:15:40]]</ref>indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used by [[Traders]] to improve their [[Speculate command|Speculates]] indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used by [[Warrior Mage]]s to improve their ability to control [[Familiars]] indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].

===Other Uses===
*Increases [[concentration]]<ref>[[Post:OOC_Thief_Meeting_Log_12.14.2008_-_12/14/2008_-_18:46:07]]</ref>
*Improves the size of experience pools, but to a lesser degree (~10% efficiency) than [[Intelligence]].<ref>[[Post:3.0 release - 01/18/2013 - 17:31]]</ref>
*Improves the ability to use [[gwethdesuans]] indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].
*Used in Spell casting indirectly by increasing [[concentration]].<ref>[[Post:Tentative Empath Magic 3.0 Spell List - 2/8/2011 - 23:27:53]]</ref><ref>[[Post:The Physiology of Spellcasting - 11/20/2009 - 14:54:15]]</ref>
*Assists with [[Athletics]]<ref>[[Post:Climbing 3.0 - 10/29/2008 - 20:41:01]]</ref>  indirectly by increasing [[fatigue]] and [[concentration]].
*Assists with [[Shield Usage skill]]
*Assists with  [[Skinning skill]]<ref>[[Post:WOTP - 11/21/2009 - 11:13:24]]</ref>
*Assists with [[Stealth]]<ref>[[Post:Stealth... - 12/09/2012 - 14:51]]</ref>
*Assists with [[Thievery_skill|Thievery]]
