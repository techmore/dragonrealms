* A TM spell against [[:Category:Extra-planar creatures|extra-planar creatures]] which includes [[Undead#Spiritual_or_Non-Corporeal|non-corporeal undead]]. Works on ghostly undead creatures like [[pale grey death spirit]]s, but not on corporeal undead like [[Faenrae reaver|faenrae reavers]]. It also works on [[Elemental_Planes#Elementals_-_Denizens_of_the_Elemental_Planes|elemental creatures]] like [[Scaly seordmaor|scaly seordmaors]] and [[Wind hound|wind hounds]]. 
* Casting this spell causes [[Hylomorphic Corruption]] and trains Sorcery and Summoning (but only when cast on extraplanar creatures that challenge the caster's skill).
* Casts have a chance to bounce. Each bounce gains a large, stacking boost to accuracy and damage.
