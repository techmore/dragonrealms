==Notes==
*Knowledge of this metaspell, or any other [[elemental weapon]] metaspell increases your weapon to tier 3 from its base of tier 2.
*Without this metaspell, roundtime reduction reaches a cap of 5 seconds, so this metaspell will result in 1 second roundtimes at cap.
*You can also {{com|PUSH}} your weapon to lower its density or {{com|PULL}} it to increase it thereby altering its weight and stats. E.g. increased density results in greater weight, more damage, and less balance. Density of summoned weapons can be either 4, 5 or 6 and they are summoned with 5 density.
