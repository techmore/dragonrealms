# Warding skill

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}

Warding is one of the new magic skills added in the [[Dragonrealms 3.0]] update which governs your ability to successfully cast and use barrier spells and abilities such as [[Ethereal Shield]] and [[Manifest Force]]. See the [[Magic_3.0|Magic page]] for details on how to best train Warding.
