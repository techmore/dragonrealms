'''Concentration''' is your character's ability to stay focused despite all distractions. A character's maximum concentration is determined using the following formula: 



+1 Concentration regeneration for every 50 points in your max pool size.  I.e. 2% of max concentration rounded down. 

Note: While sitting your Concentration regeneration goes up to 3% of max concentration rounded down.

==Formula==

Concentration = 300 + (Discipline * 1.5) + (Intelligence * 0.75) + (Stamina * 0.5)
