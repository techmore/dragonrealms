=Casting the Fissure=
*To cast this spell, one must indicate an element to base the fissure on. Valid options are: AETHER, AIR, EARTH, ELECTRIC, FIRE and WATER. (e.g. CAST FIRE)
*The amount of bonus mana a fissure generates is directly related to its size, which is determined by the amount of mana you initially put into it.
*Possible Fissure sizes are: tiny, small, <no description>, large, gaping, or enormous. (e.g. an enormous fiery fissure)
*Over the duration of the spell the fissure will shrink in size, thus lowering the boost of available mana.

=Closing the Fissure=
*The caster may {{tt|CLOSE FISSURE}} to prematurely end the spell. 

=Subsequent Casts=
*After your initial cast, with enough skill, you can bolster the fissure with additional castings. You may continue to add power and duration based upon your Utility skill and mana used up to a point when additional casts will have no effect on duration, size, or potency of the fissure.
*Do not cast with an element opposed to the one already in use (Fire <=> Water, Air <=> Earth, Aether <=> Electric) unless you intend to destroy the fissure.
*An exploding fissure will drain all of the caster's mana.

=Notes=
*Ethereal Fissure creates an additive Elemental mana boost in the room that stacks on top of the original mana level.
*When training with this spell, it may be best to first CLOSE the previous Fissure before opening a new Fissure.
*Before casting this in a room with weak mana, it may be wise to first charge your cambrinth itmes in a room with stronger mana and use the cambrinth to aid your spell.
*There may be a brief delay between casting or bolstering the fissure before the increased available mana is visible through perceiving the mana.
