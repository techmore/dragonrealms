==Notes==
*Metaspell for [[Dragon's Breath]].
*A successful hit that deals fire damage will also apply a burning effect that behaves as especially potent naphtha, capable of igniting adjacent naphtha-soaked body parts. Burn duration is heavily driven by high Intelligence, with major breakpoints at 100 and 150 Intelligence.
*A successful hit that deals fire damage will also apply [[Mark of Arhat]]. If MoA already exists on a creature target, it will spread to two additional creatures. If MoA already exists on a player target, it will be reapplied.
*Each time [[Ethereal Shield]] activates, the remaining cooldown of Dragon's Breath will be reduced by 12 sec. Getting hit by a multishot spell's 5 strikes will reduce the cooldown by 60 sec.
*Fire Lion Reborn is mutually exclusive with [[Aether Cloak]].  Its effects will be inactive under AC, and benefiting from FLR will temporarily prevent AC casts.
