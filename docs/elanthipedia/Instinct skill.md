==Usage==
Instinct is primarily trained via the {{com|SCOUT}} command. Also available is the [[Pounce ability]].

<pre>
SCOUT SYNTAX:
-------------
scout area
scout cover my tracks
scout cover <player's> tracks
scout cover my trail <direction>
scout marker
scout read learn
scout read teach
scout trailmarker
scout awareness <player>
-------------</pre>

The Instinct skill will determine the difficulty of trailmarker you are able to read.  As skill progresses more difficult trails will become available and the roundtime for scouting a trailmarker will decrease.  The amount of people you can lead down a trail will also increase with skill.

Instinct may also be trained via the {{com|TRACK}}, {{com|HUNT}}, or {{com|POUNCE}} commands.

Scouting awareness works on players, creatures you hunt, and on NPC's.

==Training strategy==
Training Instinct at low levels of skill is most easily done by mixing in {{com|POUNCE}} and {{com|HUNT}} with your normal hunting routine. In addition to training Instinct very well, {{com|HUNT}} will also train {{skill|perception}} when other players and/or critters are found within the hunting ground. It will also teach {{skill|Stealth}} if hidden when following through with a hunt to a specific creature and successfully remaining hidden, although this is less practical to do in a normal combat situation.

Once you have enough ranks to scout trailmarkers training becomes significantly easier.  Simply find two linking trailmarkers and run between the two.

Training Instinct will teach a small amount of [[perception skill|perception]]. It is possible to lock scouting and perception at the same time if the trail is of sufficient difficulty.  The longer the trail, the more Instinct skill is taught.
