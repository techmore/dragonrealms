==Notes==
* Scroll only. Cannot be permanently memorized. It is cast {{skill|Sorcery|sorcerously}}, even by guilded [[Necromancer]]s.
* Is not targeted, but uses the {{skill|Targeted Magic}}. It is {{com|prepare}}d, then {{com|cast}} at the target.
* Causes damage to the target’s skin and nerves.
* Worsens bleeders, and viciously interferes with {{com|tend}}ing. This shows up as a [[Uncurse|curse]], similar to a spell like [[Malediction]].
* The target's messaging refers to "what smells like diseased air flows past" their defenses. They also see: "Your wounds leak yellow-black ichor." Uncertain if the spell transmits ''actual'' [[Damage#Disease|disease]], or if this is simply how the curse effect manifests itself.
* Casting this spell inside a standard [[justice]] zone generates [[Social Outrage]]. [[Generates Social Outrage::true| ]]
** Casting this spell 15 times resulted in tier 1 Divine Corruption that resolved in 3 hours IRL
