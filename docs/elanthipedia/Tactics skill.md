# Tactics skill

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._


'''Tactics''' is a new skill in the Lore skillset, introduced with [[DR3]] in early 2013.  It is the skill of seeing your enemies' weaknesses and capitalizing on them.  It is contested against an opponent's [[Defending skill]].

It has three current uses: Applying multiple-opponent penalties to a target (useful for ganging up on people), using non-violent combat maneuvers, and the new combo system.  The current way to train it is to use tactical maneuvers (like {{com|weave}} and {{com|bob}}) and using {{com|analyze}} to identify [[combo]]s that apply debuffs to creatures.  Tactics trains like a weapon skill, so it must be contested against an opponent of suitable challenge.  

Notably, tactical maneuvers are favored by [[Empath]]s because they will not cause [[empathic shock]], and the skill is in their primary skillset.

==Tactical maneuvers==
*'''{{com|Bob}}''' regenerates fatigue
*'''{{com|Circle}}''' increases position and debuffs enemy balance; no fatigue cost
*'''{{com|Weave}}''' temporarily debuffs an enemy's defense in exchange for a fairly high fatigue cost
*'''{{com|Grapple}}''' enables certain [[Brawling_skill#Grappling|brawling moves]] (like '''butt''') and prevents some kinds of attacks. Using '''circle''' successfully while grappled will cause your opponent to lose balance.  However, an unsuccessful circle can backfire and harm your balance.
*'''{{com|Shove}}''' can knock opponents to the ground or to pole range.  Can also break a grapple
*'''{{com|Trip}}''' can knock opponents to the ground
If you lack the skill, some maneuvers (not {{tt|bob}}) can backfire and decrease your balance/position.
