# Reflex (stat)

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}

'''Reflex''' is a character's ability to quickly respond to actions and events going on around them.
==Uses==

===Combat:===
*Improves skill at [[evasion|evading]]
*Improves skill at blocking with a [[shield usage skill|shield]]
*Increases accuracy of certain [[Brawling skill|brawling]] commands.
*Used defensively in Reflex [[contested spells#Defending from Spells|defenses]].<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>
*Defending against [[Tactics]].<ref>[[Post:Tactics is a huge problem. - 05/11/2013 - 11:50]]</ref>


===Other Uses:===
*Assists with [[Tailoring_Discipline|Tailoring]].<ref>[[Post:Speedy tools and stats - 01/06/2012 - 10:12]]</ref>
*Probably increases weight that can be successfully juggled
