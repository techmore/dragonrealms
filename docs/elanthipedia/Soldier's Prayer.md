==2.0 Notes (May Not Still Apply)==
*Soldier's Prayer loses one minute of duration for every spell it contests.
*The spell is replaceable.
*Mana affects both duration and protection.
*The penalty for non-Paladins casting the spell is a severe reduction in mana contribution to the spell. It is more likely to blow off limbs from non-Holy casters.
*Terrible soul state no longer prevents a Paladin from casting the spell. However, a Paladin with neutral or worse soul state will be treated as "not a Paladin" and subject to the appropriate penalty.
