==Notes==
*Enables Call from Within to independently cast and maintain [[Call from Beyond]] and [[Quicken the Earth]] when either spell is missing, bypassing their ritual and corpse requirements.
*CFW's preparation time is reduced by the caster's Charisma when Puppetmaster is known.
*Zombies and dirt constructs created by Puppetmaster rely on CFW remaining active.
*Zombies created by Puppetmaster are based on the creature or material that was used to cast CFW.
*Zombies created by Puppetmaster will start with 'Offense' stance (enabling the Pounce ability) and 'Defensive' behavior (telling it to attack engaged enemies).
