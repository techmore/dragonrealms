==Notes==
* Spell's effect is stationary.
* Adds ''a writhing web of shadows'' to the objects in the room.
* Spell fails to cast when in water, for example if hunting [[Greater water sprite]]s.
* Shadow Web scrolls will drop only from [[Adan'f shadow mage]]s and [[Elder Adan'f sorcerer]]s, at a much higher rate than normal, as a {{ilink|i|fetid antelope vellum}}.
* 160 Arcana may not be enough to learn from a vellum, but if found on (or perhaps transferred via [[scroll stacker]] to) a [[Spell scrolls|simpler medium]] then 160 or lower will suffice.
