==Notes==
* Creates a shield that protects against Mind attacks such as [[Mental Blast]] and other [[:Category:Willpower contest abilities|versus Willpower]] spells.
* Protects against Nissa's Binding, the only spell that features Mind vs Fortitude (as opposed to all other Mind attacks being vs Willpower)
* Can only be cast on others by a Moonmage. Non-moonmages can only cast on themselves.
