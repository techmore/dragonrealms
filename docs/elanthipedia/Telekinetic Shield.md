Main function of TKSH is a bog-standard percentage-based damage resistance effect, albeit a relatively weak one (the synergy with [[Cage of Light]] is handy, though, and TKSH should be contested first). In addition to damage resistance based on Potency, TKSH will reduce the target time of [[Telekinetic Storm]] by up to 50% based on TKSH's Integrity.
