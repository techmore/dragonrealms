Combines the effects of [[Malediction]], [[Curse of Zachriedek]], and [[Huldah's Pall]] in a single cast, with a delay.

* [[:Category:Heavy offensive abilities|Heavy Debilitation]], with a cooldown shared with [[Time of the Red Spiral]].
* Preparation time and the delay before the curses kick in are both 16 seconds.
* 8 second roundtime on cast.
* You cannot specify offense/defense for [[Malediction]], but Beshrew will always cast the dual version of the curse with no loss of strength.
* The initial cast is not subject to anti-magic barriers; the curses themselves are.
** The curses come out in a semi-random, changing order. All three have been observed to land first and last.
==Notes==
* Scroll only spell, and scrolls can only be found from evolved [[ur-maeldryth|ur-maeldryths]].
* Observed cooldowns:
** Without Debilitation Mastery, and without being aligned to a +Debil Immortal = 5:32
** With Debilitation Mastery, and without being aligned to a +Debil Immortal = 3:32
** With Debilitation Mastery, with +Debil Immortal alignment = 2:40
