==Notes==
*Cannot be cast in extremely deep water.
*Targets must first resist a charm vs. willpower attack on each pulse in order to avoid the effect entirely. If they fail to resist then all their Targeted Magic spells until the next pulse have to break through an integrity barrier in order to be cast.
*Makes most at level creatures unable to cast targeted magic spells, since most creatures cast spells with just enough skill to be able to cast them
*You must also resist your own cast, potentially causing your own targeted magic casting to be shut down.
* Associated with Aether, but is not affected by the [[Spell_efficacy#Elemental_Efficacy|Elemental Efficacy]] system.
