==Notes==
* Can be cast in standard [[justice]] zones. [[Generates Social Outrage::false| ]]
* Can be modified by the metaspell [[Liturgy]], which will add the masking benefits from [[Rite of Contrition]] to this spell.
* Allows you to hide 1-7 [[:Category:Transcendental Necromancy spellbook|Transcendental Necromancy]] buffs from view. Transcendental buffs under this effect retain their full effectiveness, but do not appear when looking at the Necromancer or in the room (for [[Calcified Hide]]).
