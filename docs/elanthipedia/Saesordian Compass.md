==Notes==
* You need to target a single-target Lunar TM spell to benefit from Saesordian Compass in any way.
* Adds a small chance for instant lock on targeted spells
** At full mana, approximately 35% chance (sample size 100)
** Bonegrinder has a much higher chance of instant locking: 75% (sample size 115)
** Per Grejuva (10/20/2022 12:42AM #moon-mage), you cannot repeatedly target and release to get an instant lock; if it fails to instant lock, there's a 8-10 sec cooldown before it attempts again. However, it is definitely possible to get multiple back-to-back instant locks.
* The spell occasionally recasts itself when its duration ends. Releasing SCO does not cause extra teleologic corruption, despite what flavor messaging might imply.
* Causes [[Starlight Sphere]] to proc a bonus attack whenever an instant lock happens. There's a chance for a double SLS bonus attack to happen.
