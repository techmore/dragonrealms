==Notes==
* Learning this spell will kill you.
* Metaspell with three [[:Category:Blood Magic spellbook|Blood Magic]] effects:
:# [[Heighten Pain]] gains practically infinite duration but becomes limited to one target at a time. If a creature dies with it, the curse will jump to another creature in the room.
:# [[Siphon Vitality|Siphon Vitality's]] vitality healing effect is replaced by a vitality shield that is significantly more efficient per point of damage.
:::* This barrier lasts 10-40 minutes, proportional to its hit points at the time of the cast.
:::* The barrier takes up a ward slot which is freed up when the duration ends or the barrier is used up.
:::* If you do not have a ward slot avaiable when Siphon Vitality is cast without an active barrier then the original vitality healing effect occurs instead.
:# [[Blood Burst]] loses its bleeder requirement / vitality cost.
* The term alyuryn translates as "holy scorpion" in [[Gamgweth]]. Alyurynaen is the plural form.
* Like all other [[:Category:Anabasis spellbook|Anabasis spells]], this is not taught by the various guildleaders.
* Like all other Anabasis spells, this spell only functions while [[Redeemed]].
