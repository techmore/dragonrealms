==Uses==
===Backstab===
*Backstab success is based on attacker's backstab skill versus target's {{Skill|Perception}}. The higher the Perception of a creature the more difficult it is to backstab and the more experience will be gained for success.

===Ambush===
*Backstab skill is also learned when merely [[Ambush]]ing a creature rather than backstabbing it (only when ambushing a specific body part).  This is useful when fighting on quadrupedal or other non-backstabable creatures.
*The Thief may choose a specific body area to hit. The difficulty of the spot determines how well the hit lands and how much is learned. The body targets from easiest to hardest is as follows:
**Leg (left/right)
**Back
**Abdomen
**Chest
**Arms (left/right)
**Hands (left/right)
**Neck
**Head
**Eye (left/right). 
*Backstab is also factored in some guild-taught Ambush techniques.
