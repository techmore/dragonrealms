# Wisdom (stat)

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}

'''Wisdom''' represents a character's worldly knowledge, intuition and personal ability to apply life experience to various situations. Primarily affects will-based spells, strength of spirit, and the rate one learns from one's experiences.

The {{com|wisdom}} command can be used in-game to display useful information.

==Uses==

===Combat===
*Increases [[Spirit Health|spirit health]] and therefore the time before [[depart|auto-departing]] after death
*Used offensively in Mind, Magic and Spirit [[contested spells]]<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>
*Used defensively in Willpower [[contested spells#Defending from Spells|defenses]].<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>
*Increases spell damage.<ref>[[Post:3.0 release - 01/18/2013 - 14:50]]</ref>


===Guild Specific:===
*Used by [[Bard]]s for [[Recall|recalls]]<ref>[[Post:Recall 3.0 - 01/19/2013 - 11:01]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Bard Ability - Evoke - 09/29/2011 - 13:42]]</ref>
*Used by [[Clerics]] to decrease the round time when using a [[Commune]]
*Used by [[Moon Mages]] to increase prediction power and the chance of a positive prediction<ref>[[Post:Predicting in 3.0 - 01/23/2013 - 20:36]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Predicting in 3.0 - 01/24/2013 - 08:09]]</ref><ref>[[Post:An overview - 09/02/2012 - 14:02]]</ref> and increase the random chance of escaping the [[Astral Plane|Grey Expanse]] during [[Astral Travel]].<ref>[[Post:Woah, for real? - 08/13/2011 - 00:27]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Astral Plane Updates - 8/20/2011 - 00:36]]</ref>
* Used by [[Traders]] to increase [[Starlight Aura]] regeneration pulse size.

===Other Uses:===
*Increases the percentage of field [[experience]] that is transferred into real ranks (uses only base Wisdom)<ref>[[Post:Learning Question - 2/2/2011 - 1:40:28]]</ref><ref>[[Post:IMPORTANT: The EXP system, Elanthia, and you. - 5/13/2009 - 23:02:07]]</ref><ref>[[Post:So if you had to do it now... - 1/10/2010 - 11:59:39]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Uh, oh. hiding and stalking - 7/11/2009 - 17:27:32]]</ref> A player experiment concluded that increasing Wisdom from 30 to 40 improves the rate of absorption by an average of 6.24% for all skills.
*Improves accuracy when determining the [[time]].<ref>[[Post:Time Verb Updates - 07/23/2012 - 14:27]]</ref>
*Improves [[Perception skill]]
*teaching/listening to [[Scholarship skill|classes]] (small linear effect)
* Slightly decreases time to craft remedies products
