The '''Evasion skill''' is one of the three primary defensive [[page type is::skill]]s.  It is the measure of how well you can avoid attacks by dodging, and its primary stat is [[Reflex]].  Evasion is one of the most important combat skills. Your ability to evade attacks can be hurt by high hindrance and encumbrance.

==Use==
When you are attacked in combat your evasion will be checked simultaneous to your {{skill|Shield Usage}} and {{skill|Parry Ability}}, and then followed by armor.

===Advantages===
Unlike the other two defenses, Evasion is not an all or nothing defense. This means that even a failure to dodge will result in a portion of the attack being reduced, thus making it easier to resist with other forms of defense.

Also unlike the others, Evasion is almost always checked by most forms of attack. Parry is restricted to most forms of weapons, often being ignored by missiles and spells, and shield is bypassed by [[DFA]] spells.

===Disadvantages===
Evasion is the defense most affected by high burden or encumbering armor, as well as being most affected by multiple opponents.

===Control===
The degree to which Evasion is used is controlled via the {{com|stance}} command.

==Training==
