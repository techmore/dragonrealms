==Notes==
*Area effect pulsing fire/impact damage, weighted towards fire.
*Being under the effects of [[Will of Winter]] will change it to fire/cold damage weighted equally.
*Both versions cause naphtha to flare up when the spell hits a body part that is doused with naphtha. Both also increase the duration and damage of the ignited naphtha.
*The bard will appear in the room look as "<Player> who is surrounded by swirling flames."
*When using the Will of Winter form you will instead appear as "<Player> who is surrounded by an elemental maelstrom."
*Causes charges of endangering the public when used in [[Justice|justice]] areas.
