This is something the Moon Mage Guild was quick to notice and exploit in an imperfect, but useful, manner.

Ripplegate Theory allows a Moon Mage to, using equations presumably gifted by the Merelew, imprint and solve the equations necessary to teleport to any given area without need for the moons.  In practice, the magician uses Ripplegate Theory along with Braun's Conjecture to solve the enlightened math.  Afterward, the Moon Mage can cast Teleport at their "ripplegate" to return to the area with no regard to moon or moonbeams.
