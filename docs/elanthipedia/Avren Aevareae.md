==Notes==
*Requires starlight aura or a moon. [[Trader spell requirement is::auramoon| ]]
*Cast target:
:*Katamba: +Stealth, +Thievery.
:*Xibar/Yavash: -Stealth, -Thievery, light source, can pull others from hiding.
:*Aura: +Stealth (group), +Thievery (group), -Stealth (others), -Thievery (others), light source, can pull others from hiding. 
*Room wide effect.
*Will act as a light source for dark rooms when cast from starlight or on the bright moons
*Pulsing effect that can pull people from hiding when cast on a bright moon or using your aura.
