==Notes==
*Requires some level of [[Empathic Shock]] to permanently memorize from a scroll.
* IZ's{{skill|Empathy|Empathy}} exp award is proportional to your shock.
*At minimum shock, [[Icutu Zaharenela]] is esoteric in difficulty which means it is extremely difficult and mana-expensive. Deeper Shock eases both of those, down to below advanced spell levels at complete insensitivity.<ref name="8-16-2016-iz-post" />
**The Skill Range in the table above are for the esoteric levels at minimum shock.
**At complete insensitivity the pulse cost at minimum prep is 6 mana.
*No effect on constructs or undead.
*Converts the {{com|touch}} command into a {{skill|Targeted Magic}} attack ''preventing the use of {{com|touch}} to initiate an empathic link!''
*Causes minor internal wounds to your hands. If these wounds are healed they will reappear on the next pulse of the spell.
*Justice charges from this spell trigger on actual attacks via {{com|touch}}. However, it is considered "necro-looking" for the purposes of {{com|accuse}} as there is a change to the character's appearance in {{com|LOOK}} - ''{{tt|PLAYER}} has a ghoulish grey pallor, his/her hands bent into arthritic claws.'' May cause [[Social Outrage]] in towns.<ref name="8-16-2016-iz-post">[[Post:Icutu Zaharenela - 08/16/2016 - 07:54]]</ref>
*Attacks inflict shock at the same rate as any other damaging spell, but without the associated stun.
*If your {{skill|brawling}} is good enough, including any modification from [[Aggressive Stance]], then you will have reduced roundtimes. This does not improve the damage or accuracy of the touch attack.<ref name="8-16-2016-iz-post" />
*"Icutu zaharenela" is either Elvish for "aging touch," or perhaps more poetically, "touch of the old woman." This is likely a reference to [[Asketi]], [[Hodierna]]'s dark aspect, as she is often represented as an old hag or crone.
