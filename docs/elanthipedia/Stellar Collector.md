* Requires unobscured starlight, an unobscured bright moon (Xibar or Yavash), or [[Starlight Aura]] to cast. [[Trader spell requirement is::starlightaurabrightmoon| ]]
* Will only recharge from unobscured starlight.
* Duration and charge percentage show up in the active spell window.
* Charge gain happens every 100 seconds. The amount gained per pulse increases with the mana put into the spell.
* Recasting the spell will refresh the duration and retain the charge.  This can be done even with the apparatus in another room.
* Will cause a stun and damage to anyone besides the trader who tries to pick it up
* {{com|STUDY}}:You think you can PUT something tiny in the apparatus, DROP the apparatus to let it start collecting starlight, or INVOKE it to take its collected starlight.
* {{com|DROP}}:You find someplace to hide your spun glass apparatus, making sure it still sees the sky. You drop a virescent yellow spun glass apparatus.
* {{com|INVOKE}}:With an instinctive, sharp inhalation, you draw the entirety of the apparatus's collected power into your starlight aura!
* You can pick it up and drop it somewhere else at any time.
* When invoked will only consume enough charge to max out your aura.
* NOTE It appears that once the Starlight Collector spell wears off, the apparatus can still be picked up, but once you LOOK at it or manipulate it it will disappear.
