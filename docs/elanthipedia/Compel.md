*Does not affect [[Construct]]s or [[Undead]]. May enrage undead.
:Makes players or creatures leave the room the Empath is in, and prevents them from returning via some means for a while. This will be most effective on creatures, but will still have some utility versus players.<br>
:Compel will be, which is the spell which holds the former "shoo away" mechanic that used to reside in [[Innocence]]. Innocence is meant to completely prevent an [[Empath]] from being attacked by non-undead creatures so long as the Empath doesn't take aggressive actions, so it's not really possible to target it from what I am imagining. It's something that works on the Empath more than on some individual creature. Compel is what you want for crowd control.<br>
