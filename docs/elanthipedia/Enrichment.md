It expends a burst of starlight aura to supercharge some of your crystal spells for a short duration. Keep in mind that this spell is designed to convert your excess aura into powerful effects, and is quite costly. You will not be able to sustain a high uptime on it.[[Trader spell requirement is::aura| ]] 

While Enrichment is active:
* [[Trabe Chalice]] will become resistant to impact damage as well.
* Newly created [[Regalia]] will gain +1 tier to armor quality which lasts indefinitely even when Regalia is refreshed. Armor that already exists is not altered.
* [[Arbiter's Stylus]] will be prepared drastically faster.
* [[Starcrash]] will strike an additional target with each pulse.
* [[Avtalia Array]] will recharge cambrinth for three times as much.
* Grants a large damage bonus to the next [[Crystal Dart]] cast, after which the bonus will recharge over a minute as long as Enrichment is up.
All effects are dynamically applied during Enrichment's duration except for Regalia as noted above. E.g. Starcrash will gain an additional strike if Enrichment is cast during its effect and will lose a strike if Enrichment wears off during its duration.
