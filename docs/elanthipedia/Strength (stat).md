# Strength (stat)

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}

'''Strength''' is the maximum potential force a character can generate during physical activity.
==Uses==






===Combat:===
*Reduces [[Fatigue]] cost when completing combat actions.<ref>[[Post:MB stamina costs? - 01/20/2013 - 22:11]]</ref><ref>[[Post:TEST fatigue pt 1 - 6/27/2009 - 15:42:27]]</ref>
*Reduces Roundtime when swinging a weapon.<ref>[[Post:New RT Formulas - 11/22/2012 - 14:57]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Speedy tools and stats - 01/06/2012 - 10:12]]</ref>
*Improves the chance to hit foes in combat.<ref>[[Post:Combat 3.0 Update - 02/05/2012 - 06:38]]</ref>
*Increases power of physical attacks, particularly with weapons that appraise highly in the "suited for strength" category<ref>[[Post:Combat 3.0 Update - 02/05/2012 - 06:38]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Combat 3.0 Overview - 7/14/2010 - 22:28:18]]</ref>
*Improves [[Evasion]] skill indirectly by reducing [[Encumbrance]]
*Improves [[parry|parrying]] skill.<ref>[[Post:Parry and equiped weapon - 12/01/2012 - 09:49]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Parrying - 12/8/2009 - 14:59:09]]</ref>
*Improves  skill at blocking with a [[shield usage skill|shield]]
*Increases accuracy of certain [[Brawling skill|brawling]] commands.
*Used offensively in Fear and Power [[contested spells]]<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>
*Used defensively in Fortitude [[contested spells#Defending from Spells|defenses]].<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>


===Other Uses:===
* Increases maximum [[Encumbrance]] <ref>[[Post:Log, mini-meeting - 1/12/2009 - 1:31:49]]</ref>
*Increases the weight that can be dragged.
*Helps in jumping from ferry to ferry
*Affects the maximum weight of an item that Survival Primes can <tt>SLIP</tt> in and out of containers and to other people
*Reduces [[mining]] roundtimes.<ref>[[Post:Speedy tools and stats - 01/06/2012 - 10:12]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Mining RT's - 06/23/2011 - 08:13]]</ref>
*Assists with [[Athletics]] for climbing, swimming and retreating<ref>[[Post:Climbing 3.0 - 10/29/2008 - 20:41:01]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Log, mini-meeting - 1/12/2009 - 1:31:49]]</ref>
*Assists with [[Carving_discipline|Carving]] and [[Forging]].<ref>[[Post:Speedy tools and stats - 01/06/2012 - 10:12]]</ref>
