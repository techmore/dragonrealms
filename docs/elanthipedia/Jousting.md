# Jousting

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}
Jousting is a sport played by mounted warriors. Currently, the only form of jousting is tilting, the object of which is to unhorse one's opponent by riding toward him at high speed and striking him with a lance.

==Requirements and Equipment==

===Minimum Requirements===

All adventurers can joust provided that they have the proper training and equipment. Below is a list of the bare minimum training and equipment that are needed to joust:
*Ownership of a [[Horses|horse]]
*Knowledge of basic horsemanship (taught by any stablemaster)
*A lance (a pike or halberd)
*A shield (held in left hand)*
*Plate armor on the torso and head*

If a jouster is not wearing plate armor and holding a shield, he is likely to panic and leap off of his horse at the moment before impact.

===Recommended Equipment and Training===

'''Lance''': Blunted lances are available at some arenas. Although a regular lance can be used, you should avoid using lances that you don't want to lose. (Lances often break during jousts.)<br>
<br>
'''Armor''': Plate armor ''must'' be worn on the torso and head. Jousters should also consider wearing plate armor on the rest of their bodies.<br>
<br>
'''Tack and Barding''': Most jousters use a saddle and bridle to assist with their balance, positioning, and control over the horse. (Unless you are trained to ride bareback, you must use a saddle.) The mechanics do not require a saddle or barding, but if you want a more realistic experience, equip your horse with plate barding, and ride in a saddle.<br>
<br>
'''Additional Training''': Anyone who is at least 40th circle can learn how to joust more effectively, but the teacher must be a [[Paladin]] who is at least 50th circle. Horses can also be taught to perform better in jousts by [[Rangers]].

==Jousting Syntax==

To initiate the challenge, you must <tt>SIGNAL HORSE JOUST <player></tt>, and the other player will automatically be told how to respond. He has 30 seconds to agree, and if he does, the joust will begin ten seconds later.<br>
<br>
Some training fields and jousting areas include quintains to facilitate jousting practice. To use them, just <tt>GO QUINTAIN</tt> while mounted on your horse and holding your lance.

==Tournament Rules and Scoring==

Individual tournaments may vary, but in general, participants are required to use the same lances (sold at the arena), and magic is forbidden.

Tournament brackets are often divided by circle. However, this can lead to unexpected results, because most people do not train pikes or halberds as rigorously as they train their primary weapon.

'''Scoring'''

Each round is called a "pass." Participants will continue to joust until one person earns a designated number of points. The winning point total is often 5; during those tournaments, a jouster who unhorses his opponent automatically wins.

==Mechanics of Success==

For a GM statement on the mechanics, see: [[Post:Tuesday Tidings 183 - Jousting Updates - 08/22/2023 - 20:30|this update to the jousting mechanics.]]

Factors that players think affect success in jousting:
*Skill with [[Polearms skill|polearms]]
*Skill with [[Shield Usage skill|shields]]
*Skill with [[Armor Usage skill|plate armor]] (Since plate on torso and head is a required worn armor, just as shield and polearms are required to be held, plate armor skill has always been a paladin bonus. This has been verified since jousting began by comparing plate skills between winners who were an equal match in polearm and shield skills. As an armor primary guild, most Paladins have more plate skill, which when factored in, could push them into the next division.)
*Offensive and defensive stances (For best results, allocate 100 points each to shield block skill and offensive skill.)
*Whether the jouster has been taught to joust by a [[Paladin]]
*Whether the horse has been taught to perform in a joust by a [[Ranger]]
*[[Strength]], [[Reflex]], and [[Agility]] (not yet verified)
*[[Encumbrance]]/burden (not yet verified)
*Horse's gait (not yet verified)
*Armor stats are not factored into jousting scores, but you still need to be wearing it.
*Shield stats are also factored in, which is finally one area where you are objectively better off with a large shield.
*Overall armor hindrance does not factor into the checks.
*Generally going to do better with a pike than with a halberd.

==Participation by Empaths==

Empaths can participate in jousts without experiencing [[empathic shock]].
