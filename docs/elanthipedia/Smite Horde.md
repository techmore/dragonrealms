==Notes==
*Damage is weighted to slice.
*Smite Horde is very accurate at melee, moderately accurate at pole range and very inaccurate at missile range.
*Requires pure white or pristine soul state, as measured by a soulstone, to cast.
*The Sidasas Sedra ("SIS") metaspell adds fire damage and adds a 1-minute empowerment effect on the paladin if it has a target to hit; the hit does not need to connect.
*The SiS empowerment reduces the target time of each subsequent Smite Horde cast by 0 to 5 seconds, down to a 5 second minimum, based on the integrity of the preceding cast.
*The SiS empowerment also adds a TM rider (2 bird hits) to each SMITE while under its effect that's based on the potency and integrity of the last cast.
*Empowered SMITEs with a holy weapon have a moderate chance to proc a full Smite Horde cast and refresh the 1-minute empowerment.
*While empowered, some portion of fire damage caused by the TM attack is stored and can be released with a shield SLAM; the damage bonus scales with shield size. Large shields receive the largest bonus.
