===Notes===
*Can be avoided by AVOID TOUCH or DEMEANOR settings towards the empath, as a physical touch is required to cast the spell.
*Does not work on necromancers with high DO, regardless of demeanor settings.
*Works like the Cleric spell [[Vigil]]
**Drains vitality from one person to give it to whoever has lesser vitality. 
**Empaths can give up to 50% vitality, where as non-empaths can only give until about 80%. 
**Heals until 70% vitality is achieved on non-empaths (the empath cannot benefit from the vitality heal and is donor only). 
**Empaths can give in combat, but non-empaths cannot.
*A single empath can link to multiple people, including another empath who is heartlinking others.
