# Crossbows skill

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}
Combination of the [[Light Crossbow skill|light]] and [[Heavy Crossbow skill|heavy crossbow]] skills.<br />
It requires 50 ranks of [[Shield skill]] to use a crossbow with a shield (have '''not''' checked arbalests though).<br />

Adding to the widespread use of these weapon is the fact that multiple bows may be loaded and worn on the shoulder. This allows a marksman to hold up to three quick shots in reserve, and is extremely useful in PVP.

Load times are usually given as two numbers, such as 8/9, 9/10, or 10/11. The first number denotes the number of seconds of load time if the projectile is being held in the hand while loading. The second number denotes the amount of time in seconds to load if the projectile is coming from a container. Depending on a crossbow's type, the load time associated with it can be reduced if the user has sufficient skill and their stats are high enough.

The advent of the [[Tinkering discipline|Tinkering discipline]] has resulted in player-crafted crossbows that can far exceed their store bought counterparts, although for low level players the 8/9 store bought light crossbows and the museum bow recommended below are more than adequate to train with.

==Light crossbow==
The light crossbow is, as its name implies, the smaller and quicker version of the heavy crossbow. It tends to fall somewhere between its heavy cousin and the [[Bow_skill#Composite_Bows|composite bow]] in terms of power, and is only marginally slower to load than the composite bow, making it a popular choice.

Unlike its heavy counterpart, this weapon does not require the use of both hands while aiming. Thie allows the user to use a shield or weapon in the off hand while aiming.<br />The weapon relies primarily on agility to reduce its load time, making it more popular among characters who focus on agility rather than strength.

==Heavy crossbow==
The Heavy crossbow is just like a longer loading, harder hitting version of the light crossbow, but require an open hand to load and aim.<br />With high enough stats, a character can slightly reduce the hefty roundtime associated with loading a heavy crossbow. Since [[strength]] is the most significant stat for reducing load time, the heavy crossbow is sometimes favored over the light crossbow for stronger characters. <br />

==Arbalest==
The arbalest is the slowest class of bow in Dragonrealms. A arbalest type crossbow has load times starting at 18 (unhidden) and 21 seconds (while hidden) and can be loaded and fired while the off hand is full, but can '''not''' be worn.<br />

==Notable weapons==
===Light crossbow===
Classified by load times:
*'''10/11:''' The {{ilink|w|slightly rusty crossbow mounted with a ironwood goat's foot cocking handle}} from the Museum of Imperial History has a 10/11 load time.  It also compares as more suited to strength than the standard store-bought light crossbow with a 9/10 load time.
*'''9/10:''' The standard {{ilink|w|Light crossbow|store-bought light crossbow}} has a 9/10 load time. 
*'''8/9:''' The Muspar'i Trader-shop's {{ilink|w|forester's crossbow}} has an 8/9 load time as do many other crossbows, such as the {{ilink|w|Laminated oak and horn crossbow with a blued steel cranequin}} from Ilaya Taipa.  The 8/9 is so common now that few use a 9/10 crossbow.
*'''5/6:''' {{ilink|w|Simply carved maple stonebow with a bright blue leather sling|Stonebow}}s have a 5/6 loadtime, but use sling ammo instead.  Store-bought sling ammo is no good, but [[Carving_Products#Weaponry|stone ammo]] can be crafted that is considerably better.  Although they do no '''lodging damage''', some favor {{ilink|w|Stone_shard_(crafted)|stone shards}} because they are hard to lose.
