==Notes==
* Any weapon held by the aggressor (whether or not it was actually used in the assault) will be debuffed for a short time, similar in concept to the Cleric spell [[Curse of Zachriedek]].
* The debuff will trigger on any strike that is at least glancing.
* Debuffs all 3 physical damage types of a weapon.
* The debuff does not work on natural attacks from critters or brawling attacks from players and the weapon attack that triggers the debuff effect does not receive any benefit from the debuff.
* The effect cannot be triggered again on the same target until it has worn off completely from that target. There is no messaging to indicate when the debuff has worn off.
* Provides a very small amount of always on damage reduction to the caster independent of the debuff effect.
