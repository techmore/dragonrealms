==Notes==
*[[Ritual spells]] may require the use of a [[Focus (item)|ritual focus]].
*The [[Magical feats|magical feat]] Improvised Rituals is required for use of universal ritual [[Focus (item)|focii]] such as the [[Item:Lapis lazuli tree statuette|Lapis lazuli tree statuette]].
*Empath only [[attunement]] sharing (similar to [[vigil]] or [[heart link]]).
*Nearly impossible to cast without a ritual focus.
*Tree type appears to vary by Empath.
:*Known tree types: acacia, acanth, aspen, cedar, cypress, deobar, eucalyptus, fig, grapefruit, hazelnut, lemon, lunat, mikkhalbamar, mimosa, modwir, pear, peregan, poplar, rowan, sycamore, and tamarisk
*Additional Empaths may link to the tree by {{com|TOUCH}}ing the tree. The link to the tree is severed by {{tt|TOUCH}}ing it again.
*You cannot summon another tree while already linked to a tree.
*You cannot summon another tree to a room that already has one.
*You cannot summon a tree with ANY amount of shock. (excluding shock scar)
