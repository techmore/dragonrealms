==Notes==
*Will dispel a single offensive spell on the target.
*With enough mana can be used to remove a hostile debuff on the target.
**If the target is wearing a cursed item or items, this spell will affect all cursed items on that target. (Worn, or in hands)
*Will reduce the effects of Death's Sting on the target.
*Will destroy the [[giant skeletal claw]]s in [[Zaulfung]].
*Can only be cast on others as a Cleric. Non-clerics receive the message: "You strain, but lack the training to direct the Uncurse spell at others."
