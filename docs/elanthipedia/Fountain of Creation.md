==Notes==
*The amount of healing depends on the amount of mana put into the spell. The more seriously injured you are, the more mana is required for complete healing.
*Full-body healing at the cost of [[fatigue]] and [[vitality]] hits. Possible to be stunned as well: ''You collapse to the ground in exhaustion, as well as reel helplessly at the immense system shock from the sudden healing!''
*Due to difficulty of casting it's recommended for circle 60+ Empaths or 300+ PM.
*The difficulty of this spell increases with the severity of the Empath's wounds.
*There is a 60-second timer that prevents you from re-casting the spell. Messaging that the timer is up: ''You no longer feel especially strained from invoking the Fountain of Creation.''
*Spell power displayed in the flow of the energy: flowing trickles, flowing rivulets, flowing streams, gushing streams, and raging torrents.
*Amounts healed: slightly, moderately, considerably, greatly, immensely, completely.
*Heals wounds in the order the system lists them.
