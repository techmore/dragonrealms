==Notes==
* Cyclic spell with two effects that grow substantially more powerful the more [[Redeemed#Prohibited_Spells|forbidden spells]] you know up to 15.
:# Reduces the attunement cost of spellcasting but stops attunement regeneration. The cost reduction only applies to the prepped mana of a cast spell, and does not reduce the attunement costs for charging cambrinth or harnessing mana.
:# Increases the integrity of outgoing offensive spells for barrier piercing purposes, beyond the normal cap, potentially allowing you to pierce any integrity barrier. At maximum effect, it is capable of piercing any combination otherwise unpierceable barriers like [[Worm's Mist]] and [[Aether Cloak]].
* Like all other [[:Category:Anabasis spellbook|Anabasis spells]], this is not taught by the various guildleaders.
* Like all other Anabasis spells, this spell only functions while [[Redeemed]].
* This spell may only be cast with full preparation.
* Can be cast in standard [[justice]] zones. [[Generates Social Outrage::false| ]]
