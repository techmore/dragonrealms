==Notes==
*The dispel function with accompanying spirit drain will cease beyond a certain point of soul health, and increased soul purity will reduce the amount used.
*Truffenyi's Rally will attempt a dispel on each player in the group (1 spell per group member, provided there is enough potency).
*Provided the spell has enough potency, TR tries to dispel one offensive spell per member of the group; it might not be the same spell for every group member (if they're affected by different debuffs, for example).
*Potency is affected by the amount of mana you put into the spell and your spell stance.
