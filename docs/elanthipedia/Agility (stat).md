# Agility (stat)

_Automatically mirrored from Elanthipedia (2026-08-12)._

{{RTOC}}

'''Agility''' is a character's physical dexterity and working with fine motor skills.
==Uses==

===Combat:===
*Reduces Roundtime when swinging a weapon.<ref>[[Post:New RT Formulas - 11/22/2012 - 14:57]]</ref>
*Improves [[parry|parrying]] skill.<ref>[[Post:Parry and equiped weapon - 12/01/2012 - 09:49]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Parrying - 12/8/2009 - 14:59:09]]</ref>
*Improves accuracy in combat, thus increasing damage output.<ref>[[Post:Combat 3.0 Update - 02/05/2012 - 06:38]]</ref><ref>[[Post:Combat 3.0 Update - 02/05/2012 - 06:38]]</ref>
*Used to [[contested spells#Defending from Spells|defend from certain spells]].<ref>[[Post:Magic 3.0 Contested Spell Inputs - 8/7/2010 - 19:27:04]]</ref>

===Other Uses:===
*Assists with [[Athletics]] when climbing and retreating.<ref>[[Post:Climbing 3.0 - 10/29/2008 - 20:41:01]]</ref>
*Assists with [[Carving_discipline|Carving]] and [[Tailoring_Discipline|Tailoring]].<ref>[[Post:Speedy tools and stats - 01/06/2012 - 10:12]]</ref>
*Assists with [[locksmithing]]
*<s>Assists with [[mechanical lore]]</s>
*Assists with [[performance]]
*Assists with [[Skinning]] attempts<ref>[[Post:WOTP - 11/21/2009 - 11:13:24]]</ref>
*Assists with [[Stealth]]<ref>[[Post:Stealth... - 12/09/2012 - 14:51]]</ref>
